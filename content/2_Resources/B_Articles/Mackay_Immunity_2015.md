## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article studied the role of TBET and EOMES in TRM. It first showed that TRM are sufficient for skin protection independently of circulating T cells. Then, it was observed that TRM had low EOMES/TBET expresssion in a kinetic manner relative to TRM differenciation. Using OE models, there was less T cells in the skin and lower CD103 expression. This seemed linked with TGFb signaling, as TGFbRII KO had higher TBET/EOMES and TBET/EOMES had lower TGFbRII and TGFb target expression. Using partial Tbet KO (Tbet+/-) but not TBET KO, there was more TRM in the skin at late timepoint. Although TBET KO had more TRM at early timepoint, they seemed to be reduced due to decrease in survival, possibly because of defective IL15 signaling and CD122 expression. There also was a shift from IFNG+ to IL17+RORGT+. Finally, neutralizing IL15 resulted in impaired TRM formation in skin but not lung. [[TRM Phenotype]] [[TRM Infection]] [[TRM Persistance]] [[TRM Heterogeneity]] [[TRM Differenciation]] 
> 
>**Experiment** : Induction of TRM via gBT-I transfer and DNFB treatment, then depletion of circulating T cells, and infection with HSV in the DNFB-treated or control zone.
>**Results** : This resulted in CD103+ TRM formation and these cells protected against infection without circulating T cells.
>
>**Other Results** : There was no Eomes and low Tbet expression in skin gBT-I TRM after HSV infection resolution. Using spatial differences (dermis, epidermis, then CD103+ TRM) as kinetics in TRM differenciation at early timepoint, there was progressive EOMES and Tbet expression.
>**Experiment** : Forced EOMES/TBET expression and transfer of activated CD8+ T cells in mouse (which normaly is enough to generate TRM)
>**Results** : TBET OE resulted in higher CD122/CXCR3. There was less EOMES/TBET OE cells in the skin at 4/14 dpi and less CD103+ TRM.
>
>**Experiment** : OT-I WT or TGFbRII KO transfer into mouse, then infection with HSV-OVA.
>**Results** : No difference at early timepoint in cell number in total skin, but lower in epidermis and lower CD103 in TGFbRII KO. There was higher EOMES/TBET in TGFbRII KO. In EOMES/TBET OE, there was lower TGFbRII and lower TGFb-dependant protein expression (CD103, CD69, CD49a).
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of OT-I WT or TBET+/- (with lower TBET expression) into mouse, then infection with HSV-OVA.
>**Results** : There was a little more CD103+ TRM in skin in WT, and lower T cells in spleen in TBET+/-. Using Tbet KO OT-I, there was higher KLRG1- T cell number in skin, and higher skin T cell number with higher CD103 expression at 7 dpi. However, there was less at 14 dpi, and they had lower BLC2 expression, which was not the case in Tbet+/-.
>**Other Results** : Tbet-KO EOMES-OE cells did not form TRM.
>**Other Results** : There was impaired CD122 expression in Tbet KO, but not in WT or Tbet+/-. In TBET KO-EOMES low cells, there was a shift from IFNg+ to IL17+ RORgt T cells.
>
>**Experiment** : Neutralizing IL15 or IL15R injection
>**Results** : Injection diminished TRM formation.
>**Experiment** : Transfer of OT-I into mouse, then infection wit flu-VA
>**Results** : TRM had low expression of Tbet/EOMES in the lung; Tbet depletion resulted in loss of TRM in late but not early timepoint. Neutralizing IL15/IL15R antibody injection showed that lung TRM did not depent on IL15, but CD103+ lung TRM still did.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Tissue-resident memory T (Trm) cells contribute to local immune protection in non-lymphoid tissues such as skin and mucosa, but little is known about their transcriptional regulation. Here we showed that CD8+CD103+ Trm cells, independent of circulating memory T cells, were sufficient for protection against infection and described molecular elements that were crucial for their development in skin and lung. We demonstrated that the T-box transcription factors (TFs) Eomes and T-bet combined to control CD8+CD103+ Trm cell formation, such that their coordinate downregulation was crucial for TGF-β cytokine signaling. TGF-β signaling, in turn, resulted in reciprocal T-box TF downregulation. However, whereas extinguishment of Eomes was necessary for CD8+CD103+ Trm cell development, residual T-bet expression maintained cell surface interleukin-15 (IL-15) receptor β-chain (CD122) expression and thus IL-15 responsiveness. These findings indicate that the T-box TFs control the two cytokines, TGF-β and IL-15, which are pivotal for CD8+CD103+ Trm cell development and survival.
