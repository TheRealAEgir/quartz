> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article used congetically marked memory T cells and observed a TRM population which re-circulated. By doing a skin graft, there was re-circulating T cells in the blood circulation which had a TRM-like phenotype. This was also found after depleting congenically marked circulating and reactivation. This mecanism depend on S1P. WGBS showed that TRM retained some plasticity, and that TRM ressembled TEM/TCM at an epigenetic level, while ressembling more closely to effector T cells at a transcriptionnal level. Transfer of sorted TCM/TEM/TRM/Naive showed thatall had CD62L upregulation meaning they could give rise to TCM. Interestingly, ex-TRM TCM retained TRM features including Ly6C and CCR9 expression. Still, TCM made more TCM in the spleen and TRM made TRM in the organ of predilection. Transfer of exTRM showed that they still go the predilected organ, meaning this is kept after de-differenciation. exTRM only migrated after infection and activation. >**Experiment** : Thy1.1 (CD90.1) OT-I TRM generated in skin via VSV-OVA infected mouse, and transplantation of the skin in infection matched CD45.1 OT-I containing immune mouse, then reactivation by local peptide injection.
>**Results** : There was Thy1.1 cells observed in dLN, as well as Thy1.1+ TCM and TEM in distant LN. Depletion of these cells via intravascular depleting antibody injection and re-activation resulted in the same observation, in skin and in FRT. Recirculating exTRM had TRM-like phenotype CD103+CD49a+Ly6C+ KLRG1-CD62L-. S1P seems to contribute to this mechanism, because there was less CD69+ LN TRM after gp33 rechallenge of P14 memory T cells in LCMV-immune mouse.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of naive P14 T cells in naive mouse, then infection with LCMV Armstrong, and sort of MP, TE, TEM, TCM, TRM and naive T cells based on phenotype and RNA-seq, WGBS
>**Results** : TEM and TCM transcriptionnaly ressembled and shared more similarity with naive than with TRM. By contrast, PCA analysis of WGBS showed that naive, TE/MP and memory T cells grouped in 3 distinct areas. Using an algorithm that predict plasticity based on naive and terminally exhausted as reference, TRM had a mean score between TCM and TEM, suggesting it retained some plasticity.
>
>**Experiment** : Sorting of TEM, TCM and TRM and transfer in naive mouse then infection with LCMV
>**Results** : Blood cells upregulated CD62L upon activation except for TEM. Ex-TRM downregulated CD69 and CD103, but expression of Ly6C and CCR9 stayed the same, suggesting that TRM can de-differenciate into a TCM with some TRM-like features. TCM produced more spleen memory T cells, and TRM had predilection tower intestine (both LP and epithelium), the tissue from where they where extracted, but not other NLTs such as SG and FRT.
>
>**Experiment** : Sorting of P14 T cells subtypes after LCMV, transfer into a host, reinfection, sorting of ex-TCM/ex-TRM, transfer into a host, reinfection, and sorting of TRM/ex-TRM for epigenetic analyses.
>**Results** : Tertiary exTRM had TRM capacities (repopulation to the organ of predilection and reaquire of phenotype), meaning that the initial is kept, even after two de-differenciation. In the absence of re-stimulation, no ex-TCM or ex-TRM are recovered in the small intestine.
>
>**Other Results** : After *in vitro* stimulation with IL2/IL15/TGFb, exTRM had the highest upregulation of CCR9 and Ly6C.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Central memory T (TCM) cells patrol lymph nodes and perform conventional memory responses upon re-stimulation: proliferation, migration, and differentiation into diverse T cell subsets while also self-renewing. Resident memory T (TRM) cells are parked within single organs, share properties with terminal effectors, and contribute to rapid host protection. We observed that reactivated TRM cells rejoined the circulating pool. Epigenetic analyses revealed that TRM cells align closely with conventional memory T cell populations, bearing little resemblance to recently activated effectors. Fully differentiated TRM cells isolated from small intestine epithelium exhibited the potential to differentiate into TCM, TEM, and TRM cells upon recall. Ex-TRM cells, former intestinal TRM that rejoined the circulating pool, heritably maintained a predilection for homing back to their tissue of origin upon subsequent reactivation and a heightened capacity to re-differentiate into TRM cells. Thus, TRM cells can rejoin the circulation but are advantaged to re-form local TRM when called upon.
