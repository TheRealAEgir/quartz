## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article studied langerhans cells/TRM in the skin in response to TGFb maturation by keratinocytes, and found that these cells depend on TGFb signaling to reside. TGFb is synthtized bound to LAP, and activation by LAP-TGFb clivage by alphaVbeta6 or alphaVbeta8 is necessary. The constitutive TGFb signalisation lead to less LC in the LN, and removing ITGB6 (KO or neutralizing antibody) lead to spontaneous LC migration in WT but not TGFb constituve signaling mouse. ITGB6 is expressed by keratinocytes in the IFE/bulge skin region, and ITGB6 KO had effect on LC in the IFE regions. Removal of ITGB6 in the LC had no effect. ITGB8 is expressed by keratinocytes in the IM regions, and removal of ITGB8 in the keratinocytes but not on the LC had effect in LC number in skin. TGFb signaling was validated in these model using rapporter tool. UV initiate LC migration to LN, and it was found that UV lead to decrease in ITGB6/8 expression adn TGFb signaling. UV migration was stopped in TGFb constituve signaling LC mouse. Finally, TRM were also affected by ITGB6/ITGB8 removal (KO/neutralizing ab) in the skin (P14 + LCMV + DNFB), were the number of recruted effector was not affected. CD103 expression was lowered. Similar results in intestine epithelium (only ofr ITGB6, ITGB8 is not expressed) but not in lamina propria. [[TRM Caracteristics]] [[TRM Differenciation]]  [[TRM Heterogeneity]]
> 
>Results : In human patient treated with losartan (which inhibitit TGFb signaling via unknown mecanism), the concentration of langherhans cells (LC) is decreased.
>
>**Experiment** : Use of huLangerin-CreER T2 with loxP-STOP followed by constituvely active TGFbRI mouse.
>**Results** : The treatment with tamoxifen result in TGFb-signaling LC. This lead to less LC in the LN suggesting less homeostatic migration. Removing of TGFb signaling by ITGB6 KO o r neutralizing antibody(which lead to removal of TGFb maturation and permnanently inactivated LAP-bound TGFb) lead to spontaneous migration, only in WT but not the constitutive TGFb signaling LC.
>
>**Results** : alpha V beta 6 which is important in the maturation of TGFb is expressed by keratinocytes, particularly on some skin region (IFE/bulge). Interstingly, LC are recruited  an are found in IM region with no ITGB6 kertatinocytes.
>**Results** : ITG6 KO mouse showed no differences in LC in the IM region, but there was less LC in the IFE regions.
>
>**Experiment** : To confirm that ITGB6 from the keratinocytes and not endogenous LC ITGB6 is important to this phenomenon, bone marrow chimera (WT->WT and WT->ITGB6 KO)
>**Results** : There was WT LC in skin from WT->WT, but ~50% WT->ITGB6 confirming no role in endogenous LC ITGB6. Transfer of WT in ITGB6 KO mouse had the same effect than ITGB6 KO mouse on the LC skin localisation.
>
>**Experiment** : ITGB8 have similar role to ITGB6 in TGFb maturation, and is expressed by skin keratinocytes from the regions that do no express ITGB6 (that is, in IM regions). To study this, use of mouse with specific ablation of ITGB8 in LC (via huLangerin) or keratinocytes (via keratin 14).
>**Results** : ITGB8 ablation in LC had no effect on LC presence in skin, but ITGB8 ablation in keratinocytes lead to decreased number of LC in the IM regions but not in IFE regions. Double ITGB6/ITGB8 KO mouse had no LC in the skin.
>
>**Experiment** : Use of a *in vitro* assay for activated TGFb; TGFb-responsive reporter expression of luciferase
>**Results** : Antibody blockade of alphaVbeta6 in WT ITGB8 KO keratinocytes lead to decrease of luciferase. In intestine epithelium were there is no ITGB8 expression, alphaVbeta6 neutralizing antibody treatment lead to depletion of CD103+ DC.
>
>**Experiment** : LC migrate (to LN ?) after UV migration. TGFb constitutive signaling mouse or WT was subjected to UV (B)
>**Results** : There was no more LC in the skin in UVB treated WT, but they remained in TGFb contistutive signaling mouse. In these conditions, LC had higher CD86 and lower CCR7. Keratinocytes had lower ITGB6/ITGB8 expression after UV treatment, and lower active TGFb signaling with luciferase reporter.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of P14 T cells in WT/ITGB6 KO/ITGB6 KO + ITGB8 KC specific KO mouse and infection with LCMV + DNFB
>**Results** : TRM number in the skin was negatively affecter by the absence of TGFb maturation. Absence of LC did not had an effect on TRM. This did not had an effect on T cells entry as there was no differences at 7 dpi. Expression of CD103 was lower. Neutralization of ITGB6 at memory timepoint lead to decrease in the number of TRM.
>**Other Results** : Similar results in the epithelium of the intestine with ITGB6 absence, but not in lamina propria.

> > The cytokine TGF-β maintains the residency of cells of the immune system in barrier tissues. Kaplan and colleagues demonstrate that specific integrins expressed by epithelial cells activate latent TGF-β and that this is critical to maintain residency of cells of the immune system in the skin and gut.
