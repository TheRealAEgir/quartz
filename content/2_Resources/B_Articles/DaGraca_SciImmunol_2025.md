## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This paper identified ID3 as a key player in Tpex cells. This gene was first identified by scRNAseq of mouse infected with LCMV Arm/Cl13 and studi of the MP cluster. Then, it was found that only ID3+CXCR6- but not ID3-CXCR6+ are pluripotent. This population was also sorted using CD62L+ID3+ and cD62L+ID3- vs CD62L-, showing that only ID3+ formed ID3+ cells. When transferind ID3 KO or OE cells, it was observed that ID3 had a role in cell expansion and formation of TCF1+ T cells, especially in chronic infection. RNAseq of ID3 KO/WT in Tex/Tpex confirmed this, and hinted that only ID3+ could express KIT. RNAseq and transfer of isolated KIT+, CD62L-, CD62L+ showed that CD62L+ are the most pluripotent, and that KIT may represent a transient state between Tpex and Tex. Cytokine screening identified IL36b and IL18 as enhancing the proliferation of the ID3 population after TCR peptide activation. Finally, ID3 KO mouse had reduced tumor control, and pre-treating T cells with IL36b/IL18 enhanced tumor control. [[Effector_Memory cell formation]] [[Tpex]]
> 
>**Experiment** : scRNAseq of P14 T cells from mouse infected with LCMV Arm 8/21 dpi or Cl13 21 dpi.
>**Results** : 3 clusters from Cl13 : Tpex, effector-like (CX3CR1) and Tex (CD101). 2 clusters from Arm : effector and memory. There was a MP cluster from Arm, and another Tpex from Cl13 which contains some cells from Am. Tpex expressed CD62L/CCR7/ID3/TCF1/CD127/CD95 and repressed CCR2/CXCR6/ID2 PD1/TOX.
>**Other Results** : These populations were also found in FC with ID3 reporter P14 T cells
>
>**Experiment** : Isolation and transfer of CXCR6+ID3-, CXCR6-ID3+ and KLRG1+ Teff from LCMV Arm (or ID3+ Tpex from Cl13) in mouse infected with LCMV
>**Results** : Only MP had CD127 expression. CD62L fraction was larger from ID3+ and only these expressed ID3. While CXCR6+ fraction remained CXCR6+ and did not expressed ID3, ID3+ had a CXCR6+ fraction, suggesting that ID3+ fraction is more pluripotent than CXCR6+.
>
>**Other Results** : In CD62L+ from mouse and human data, there is two populations that can be distinguished based on CXCR6 and ID3 expression.
>
>**Experiment** : Isolation of CD62L+ID3+, CD62L+ID3- and CD62L- from LCMV Arm mouse, transfer into naive mouse, then infection with LCMV Arm/Cl13.
>**Results** : CD62L had low re-expansion potential. Cells from ID3+ had the highest IL2 production. Only ID3+ formed ID3+TCF1+ in Cl13 infection with no impact on CX3CR1+ formation.
>
>**Experiment** : ID3 GFP/GFP KO mouse vs ID3 GFP/+ P14 T cells transfered into naive mice, then infection with LCMV Arm/Cl13.
>**Results** : ID3 KO were impaired in expansion, especially in chronic infection. There was less TCF1+ T cells as well; effector cell were less impacted.
>**Experiment** : co-transfer of WT/ID3 OE
>**Results** : More expansion in ID3OE and more Tex with no alteration in Tpex; by 20 dpi, there was no more difference in chronic infection.
>**Experiment** : E2A OE (since ID3 inhibit E2A)
>**Results** : Similar result to ID3 KO.
>
>**Experiment** : RNAseq of ID3KO, Tpex and Tex at 5 dpi.
>**Results** : ID3 KO lead to reduced proliferation and enhanced inflammation/apoptosis genes vs Tpex + CD62L, MYB, CCR7 and decreased HELIOS, IRF4, PD1, KIT.
>**Experiment** : RNAseq of ID3KO/WT on CD62L+/CD62L-KIT- Tpex.
>**Results** : Less proliferation in ID3 KO + metabolic status genes.
>**Experiment** : RNAseq of KIT+; CD62L+; CD62L- Tpex
>**Results** : CD62L+ and KIT+ were the most different and shoed cell cycle and metabolism genes. After transfer into another mouse, CD62L are more pluripotent.
>
>**Experiment** : cytokine screen of ID3GFP P14 T cells with gp33 peptide presence.
>**Results** : IL-36β, IL-7, IL-15, IL-18, IL-2, IL-36α or IL-33 was most potent in promoting the generation of TCF1+ T cell. Only IL-1 cytokine family members, including IL-36β, IL-18, IL-36α and IL-33, promoted the generation of T cells expressing ID3 in addition to TCF1.
>
>**Experiment** : Evaluation of tumor control of ID3 KO/WT and with IL36b treatement.
>**Results** : ID3 KO showed reduced tumor control.  OT-I pre-treated with IL36b or IL18 were more efficient in tumor control.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Stem-like T cells are attractive immunotherapeutic targets in cancer patients given their ability to proliferate and differentiate into effector progeny. Thus, identifying T cells with enhanced stemness and understanding their developmental requirements is of broad clinical and therapeutic interest. Here, we demonstrate that during acute infection, the transcriptional regulator ID3 identifies stem-like T cells that are uniquely adapted to generate precursor of exhausted ‘Tpex’ cells in response to chronic infection or cancer. Expression of ID3 itself enables Tpex cells to sustain T cell responses in chronic infection or cancer, while loss of ID3 results in impaired maintenance of CD8+ T cell immunity. Further, we demonstrate that IL-1 family members including IL-36β and IL-18 promote the generation of ID3+ T cells that mediate superior tumor control. Overall, we identify ID3 as a common denominator of stem-like T cells in both acute and chronic infections that is specifically required to sustain T cell responses to chronic stimulation.
