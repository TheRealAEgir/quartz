## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article used bioinformatic analyses of HGSOC and describe a TRM-like stem-like populatio which is important for the prognosis. Using trogocytosis as a way to discrimine antigen-specific TILs, it was observed that tumor-antigen specific TILs exists in HGOSC and that it express exhaustion associated genes. Using mouse OVA-UPK10 with OT-I, it was found that TRM-like were better than Trecirc at delaying tumor growth. scRNAseq from human sample TRM sorted cells showed TRM heterogeneity and a possible TRM differenciation trajectory from TRMstem to TRMeff, TRMprol and TRMexh. TCR sequence showed that a low proportion (~3% of total TCR sequence) were important and shared with TRMstem. Classification was validated using FC and ATACseq, and TCR reactivity was validated using TCR cloning into jurkat cells and autologous co-culture. IHC showed higher predictive value of TRM and in particular TCF1+ TRM cells over TCF1-, and that TRM were specifically found in tumor bed. Finally, using antigen-pulsed dendritic cells activation, it was found that only activated OT-I accumulated in tumor beds, and using low affinity antigen that antigen affinity drive TRMstem (high affinity) vs TRMeff (low affinity) differentiation. >**Experiment** : Use of trogocytosis (transfer of membrane by antigen-activated T cells) and EPCAM+ T cells to detect antigen-specific CD8+ T cels in human HGSOC
>**Results** : EPCAM+ CD8+ T cells expressed CD103/CD69. Transcriptomic analysis vs TCRIC CD103- showed exhausted phenotype including PD1/LAYN/CTLA4/TIGIT/TOX/TIM3, IFNG/GZMB, CXCL13, CXCR6 and low level of TCF1/IL7R/CCR7/CD28. Pathways included repeated antigen stimulation, mitotic cell cycle epxansion, clonal expansion, tumor hypoxia.
>
>**Experiment** : OT-I transfer into mouse with OVA-UPK10 ovarian tumor bearing mouse.
>**Results** : Only mouse with OVA aquired a TRM-like phenotype. Activation pre-transfer increase the accumulation of CD69+CD103+CD8+ TILs.
>**Experiment** : Transfer of TRM-like and Trecirc from donor mouse in recipient mouse and tumor growth measurement
>**Results** : TRM-like were more effective at delaying tumor growth.
>
>**Experiment** : scRNAseq-TCRseq of sorted CD8+CD69+CD103+ TRM-like sorted from 4 HGSOC samples.
>**Results** : 5 clusters group bsaed on RNA and TCR similarity, and trajectory from recently activated to TRMstem TCF1lowCD127posCCR7posCD38neg, then GZMB+PRF1+GNLY+ZNF683+ effector TRM-like T cells (TRMeff), then TIGITlowHAVCR2+/lowHLA-DRhighENTPD1+/low TOX+ TILs proliferative TRMprol then PD-1high HAVCR2highTIGIThighENTPD1highHLA-DR+TOX+ cells (TRMexh). This was somewhat validated with cytometry protein expression and ATAC co-expression. ETV1 increased through TRMeff -> TRMexh and GZMK decreased from TRMstem. ZNF/KLF/SP FT family were enriched in TRMstem and POU/FOX/HOX were enriched in TRMexh.
>
>**Other Results** : 13% of Trecirc TCR sequence were shared with TRM, and 9,2% with TRMstem, and had a difference transcriptome from other Trecirc.
>
>**Experiment** : Multiomic scRNA-ATAC-TCRseq from 8 HGSOC samples sorted TRM-like
>**Results** : There was TCF1+ TRMstem with lower CD103 that expressed CD49a/RUNX3/LFA-1, and CD127, CD28, CCR7. There was TCR sharing among TRM populations.
>**Other Results** : TCR tumor-antigen reactivity was confirmed by TCR transduction.
>
>**Experiment** : IHC
>**Results** : high TRM-like was associated with improved OS. CD3+ TILs only was associated fro intra-epithelial lymphocytes. TRM were located inside tumor islet, whereas circulating were found in all stroma and tumor. TCF1+CD103+CD3+CD8+ was strongly associated with outcome, but not TCF1+CD103-CD69+-, and TCF1-CD103+ TRMexh were predictive only when there was no TRMstem. CLEC9A+ DC were found around Trecirc but not TRM.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of OT-I activated by antigen-pulsed dendritic cells prior to transfer
>**Results** : Cells expressed CD69 prior to transfer. Transfered cell accumulated at OVA+ tumor beds only when activated, and antigen concentration did not change the TRMstem/TRMeff ratio.
>**Experiment** : Use of low affinity Q4H7 and G4 peptide
>**Results** : Low affinity reduced accumulation at tumor beds and TRM-like differentiation and less TCF1 expression TRMstem while more TRMeff. In tumor without OVA, the ratio of TRMstem/TRMeff was diminished.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Despite repeated associations between T-cell infiltration and outcome, human ovarian cancer remains poorly responsive to immunotherapy. We report that hallmarks of tumor recognition in ovarian cancer-infiltrating T-cells are primarily restricted to tissue-resident memory (TRM) cells. Single-cell RNA/TCR/ATAC sequencing of 83,454 CD3+CD8+CD103+CD69+ TRM cells and immunohistochemistry of 122 high-grade serous ovarian cancers shows that only progenitor (TCF1low) tissue-resident T-cells (TRMstem cells), but not re-circulating TCF1+ T-cells, predict ovarian cancer outcome. TRMstem cells arise from transitional re-circulating T-cells, which depends on antigen affinity/persistence, resulting in oligoclonal, trogocytic, effector lymphocytes that eventually become exhausted. Therefore, ovarian cancer is indeed an immunogenic disease, but that depends on ~13% of CD8+ tumor-infiltrating T-cells (~3% of CD8+ clonotypes), which are primed against high-affinity antigens and maintain waves of effector TRM-like cells. Our results define the signature of relevant tumor-reactive T cells in human ovarian cancer, which could be applicable to other tumors with unideal mutational burden.
, Anadon et al demonstrate that human ovarian cancer-infiltrating T-cells exhibiting hallmarks of sustained and active tumor antigen recognition are restricted to ~13% of CD8+ T-cells at tumor beds, corresponding to ~3% of CD8+ clonotypes. These clonotypes represent TRM lymphocytes with a reservoir that retains features of stemness.
