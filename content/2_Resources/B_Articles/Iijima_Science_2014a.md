## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article used varignal HSV immunization are described a protective immunity against re-challenge lead by CD4 T cells, but not by CD8 or B cells [[TRM Infection]]. Only intravaginal immunization confered protection [[TRM Therapies]]. CD4 T cells were localized in memory lymphocytes clusters (MLC) that was distinct from TLS [[TRM Localization]]. They had a TRM phenotype with low CD103 [[TRM Phenotype]]. This population did not relied on circulating t cells form maintenance [[TRM Persistance]]. When doing a parabiosis with a immunized mouse and a naive mouse, only the immunized mouse is protected from lethal dose challenged, further confirming the role of TRM [[TRM Infection]]. CD4 T cells from MLC had high TCR expansion, suggesting HSV specificity [[TRM Specificity]]. This population relied on CCL5 (and CD8 TRM on CXCL9) for recruitement [[TRM Migration]]. CCL5 was produced by CD11b+MHCII+ macrophages which helped CD4 to produce IFNg, even though there also was a IFNg constituve population.
> 
>**Experiment** : HSV immunization and re-challenge
>**Results** : HSV immunization lead to protective immunity against re-challenge. This required CD4 T cells and IFNg secretion, but not CD8 or B cells. Only intravaginal immunization conferred protection through enough CD4 T cells. 
>
>**Experiment** : HSV immunization in parabiotic mice
>**Results** : There was CD4 T cells in host localized in memory lymphocyte clusters (MLC), and donor was at periphery. CD8 were found in the MLC and epithelium, and rarely in periphery. MLC were distinct from TLS as there was no PNAd+ and LYVE-1+ vessels.
>
>**Other Results** : CD4+ TRM were CD62L− CD44hi CD11a+ CD69+ CD49d+ CCR7, with no KLF2 and only a small CD103+ subset.
>
>**Experiment** : FTY720 treatement to test if this population rely on recruitement
>**Results** : There was no difference in HSV-specific IFNg+ CD4+ and gB tetramer+ CD8+ T cells, suggesting that these does not rely on circulating cells for maintenance.
>
>**Experiment** : Re-challenge with letal dose in parabiotic mouse in the donnor that have TRM (immune), or in the host that only have TCIRC from the immunized donnor (naive)
>**Results** : Mouse with TRM was protected from the re-challenge, but not mouse with TCIRC only. There was some IFNg secretion in immune-derived TCIRC in the naive mouse after 5 days. This delay was hypothetized to be the main reason for the non-proctection.
>
>**Experiment** : Laser capture microdissection to extract CD4 from the MLC and TCR sequencing
>**Results** : There was some sharing in MLC and LN CD4 T cells at the peak of the response and some dominant clone, probably specific for HSV.
>
>**Experiment** : Pertissis toxin treatment to inhibit chemotaxie and anti-CCL5/CXCL9
>**Results** : Treatment resulted in loss of CD4 TRM in MLC and proctection against re-challenge. CD4 TRM expressed CCL5, CXCL9, CCR1, CCR5, CXCR3. Anti-CCL5 reduced HSV-specific CD4 and CD8 and collapsed MLC, while anti-CXCL9 reduced CD8 TRM number.
>
>**Experiment** : Experiments to determine CCL5 producer
>**Results** : IF and cytometry showed that CCL5 was mainly produced by CD11b+ macrophage. Depletion of CD11b (inducible) cells made MLC disappear and reduced CD4 TRM within 5 days. These cells were required for IFNg production in CD4 T cells but there was a population that was expressed constitutive IFNg.
> %% end Notes %%
