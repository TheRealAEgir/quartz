## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%
>**Summary** : Use a mouse model with HSV infection and described an antigen-specific CD103/CD49a/CD62Llow/CD122low CD8+ population in the skin and ganglia graft. These cell do not exit the graft, and localise preferentially in some skin compartment. They are long lived without antigen stimulation.
>[[TRM Historic]] [[TRM Phenotype]] [[TRM Effector]] [[Method]] 
> 
> **Experiment** : Transplanting ganglia into mice with circulating HSV-specific CD8+ memory cells obtained with prealable infection. T cells from the transplant itself (GFP+) are resident and can be diffenrenciated appart from the circulating T cells (CD45.1+). Then, a second systemic HSV infection is used.
> **Model** : gBT-I.GFP-transgenic mice (all T cells are specific for the gB antigen from HSV and also express GFP)
> **Results** : Almost all gB-specific T cells responing to the re-challenge originated from the newly recruited pool, meaning that resident T cells did not leave the graft.
> To show that resident T cells do lack the re-circulation capacities but are still capable of mounting, the graft was transplanted into a naive mice together with a fresh latent graft. Only recruited cells entered the fresh graft.
> 
> **Experiment** : Infection in the skin of the flank
> **Results** : There is antigen-specific cells that stay if the same flank as the origin of the infection and do not migrate to the other flank.
> 
> **Experiment** : Transfert of gBT-I and OT-I cells in the flank skin and HSV infection
> **Results** : Both gBT-I and OT-I stay long term, with a modest antigen bias. This indicated that the phenomenom is no only due to inflammation.
> **Results** : Histologic examination showed CD103+ T cells at the site of the infection. These cells were CD62Llow/CD122lowCD69high/CD49ahigh. There was less homeostatic proliferation than Tcirc
> 
> **Experiment** : Transplantation of HSV-recovered and naive graft into naive skin
> **Results** : There was tissue resident T cells that lived in absence of antigen.
> 
> **Experiment** : Infection of mice with site that contain more TRM in a B cell deficient mice
> **Results** : Those site are more protected than the other sites. Additionnal depletion of CD4 or CD8 with antibody suggested a T cell dependent control. CD4+ antibody had the greater effects. Also in RAG KO mouse.
