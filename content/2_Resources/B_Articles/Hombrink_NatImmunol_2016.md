## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article used transcriptomics analyses and described lung CD103+ TRM (for 4 entire figures). They observed quicker IFNg secretion after stimulation compared to blood T cells. At last, they found higher expression of NOTCH1 and NOTCH/RPBJ pathways. They were able to show that KO of NOTCH1/2 in mice resulted in lower number of TRM expressing less CD103. They were able to replicate these results by inhibiting NOTCH signaling (using gamma-secretase inhibitors). Transcriptome analyses of g-secretase inhibitors treated CD69+ lung TRM didn't revealed a loss in TRM gene appart for CD103, but there was a few diferences including CD25, MTORC1 pathway and transporters [[TRM Differenciation]] [[TRM Phenotype]] [[TRM Effector]].
> 
>**Experiment** : Analyses of CD45RA- blood and lung tissue CD8 T cells + transcriptomic
>**Results** : Lung CD8 T cells expressed CD103 but low CD27. CD103+ T cells also expressed CD103. Transcriptomic analyses revealed that most heterogeneity came from tissue, even blood CD103+/lung CD103+ and lung CD103-/blood CD103-. Lung CD103+ had high CD103, KLRC1, some HSPs, EGR2, FOSB, ATF3, RBPJ, XCL1, CXCR6, FASLG, PHLDA1, BIRC3, TRAF1, TANK, CD97, IFNg and low S1PR1, CD62L, KLRG1, KLF12 and GZMM. Pathways analyses revealed differences in cytokines signaling, chemokines receptor signaling, active transport of ions and small molecules, apoptosis, TNF receptor signaling and the proinflammatory immune response. TRM had glucose deprivation signature and hypoxia (and high expression of HIF1A/HIF2A). Chemokines receptors included CXCR3, CXCR6, CCR5, CCR6 and downregulation of CX3CR1. Adhesion molecules included ITGAV, CD44, CD97, VCAM1 and CLDN18. They had constitutive mRNA expression of IFNg, TNF, GZMB. Chemokines included CCL3, CCL4, CCL20, XCL1. Inhibitory receptors and TF included CTLA4, PD1, BTLA, LAG3, SPRY1, A2AR, TWIST1 and BACH2. Transcription factors included downregulation of Tbet and EOMES, and upregulation of NOTCH1/RBPJ and NFkB associated, RUNX3, BATF, AHR, ETS1, EPAS1, IRF4, NFkB1, NFkB2, REL, NFAT5, RELA) and no differences in HOBIT/BLIMP1 compared to blood. Gene signatures of TF lead to HIF1a and NOTCH1 being the most upregulated.
>
>**Experiment** : ex-vivo culture with notch ligand
>**Results** : There was upregulation of IFNg in lung TRM.
>
>**Experiment** : Conditionnal NOCH1/2 KO via CD4-cre, study of TRM with anti CD8b intra vascular labelling.
>**Results** : There was fewer TRM in the lung which expressed lower CD103, despite similar total number of lung T cells. In an influenza infection model, there also was less antigen-specific TRM.
>**Experiment** : Treatment of inluenza-infected mice with g-secretase inhibitors (g-secretase is the one which is cleaving the membrane NOTCH for signaling).
>**Results** : Same results as previous. Transcriptomics analyses showed that the treatment affected CD69+ lung T cells, but not CD69-. Appart from downregulation of CD103, most gene were not of the TRM transcriptome, like CD25 and mTORC1/glycolysis pathways, LFA1, THY1, transporters for various metabolites.
>
