## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article used barcoding technology to access for TRM precursor inside the Teff pool, and found that there is an early comitment in Teff that take place in the blood and that define TE, TRM and TCIRCM fate. 7% of the clones produce ~50% of the total TEFF pool in blood, and each clone have similar capacity to disseminate in each organ. Most clones contributed to both TRM and TCIRCM, but about 50% had a bias toward either population without contribution in clonal Teff burst size. This observation could not be explained by stochastic survival or engraftment, meaning that Teff clones should have intrinsic differences. ScRNAseq revealed Teff population, with MP ressembling TRM and TCIRCM. Teff clones also had non-stochastic differentiation toward TE or MP, and production of MP TRM-like by clones predicted the production of TRM by the same clone, and similarly for TCIRCM and MP TCIRCM. Finally, using vaccination at two different skin site; the two site had similarities in clone composition; delaying one vaccination lead to similar results; there was more similary in TRM between site that to TCIRCM in term of clonal composition. [[TRM Precursors]] [[TRM Specificity]] [[TRM Differenciation]]
> 
>**Experiment** : BC2.0 barcoding of naive thymocytes and intrathymical transfer to generate naive barcoded OT-I GFP+ T cells, and transfer of these cells in a recipient mouse, then vaccication with OVA-coding plasmid.
>**Results** : barcode-labeled TRM were detected in tissue at late time point and expressed CD69 and CD103. Retrieving of T cell subtypes at the peak of the effector phase and quantifiyng DNA barcode revealed : 7% of the clones produce ~50% of the total TEFF pool in blood. Most clones were TEFF in all examined tissues, and clones were corralated between tissues, suggesting that naive T cells have similar capacity to disseminate throughout the body.
>
>**Experiment** : Day 12 Teff vs day >60 memory clones
>**Results** : Most naive clones contributed to both TCIRCM and TRM memory formation, but most clones had bias toward either TCIRCM or TRM by a factor of ~12. About 50% of clones preferentially contributed to either, with ~30% for TRM and ~17% for TCIRCM. There was no contribution of TEFF clone burst size toward either of the memory type.
>
>**Other Results** : During memory formation, clones undergo substantial changes in clonal hierarchy. This could not be recapitulated by simulation of the data, suggesting that this was not due to stochastic survival of engraftment and maybe this is due to intrinsic differences in clones.
>
>**Experiment** : scRNAseq in the same settings
>**Results** : TEFF had heterogeneity according to CD127 and KLRG1. Groupind meta cell based on some known genes (CD127, CD27, CD62L, CD28, CXCR3, KLRG1, KLF2, TBET, CX3CR1, ZEB2) resulted in 3 groups (TE, MP, int). Then, use of TRM vs TCIRCM genes separated MP in two (TCIRCM-like CD62L/CXCR5/KLF3/EOMES and TRM-like CD103/CD49a/FABP5). TRM-like MP also expressed CD101, AHR, CCR10, CXCR6, CD25 and TGFbRI.
>**Other Results** : TEFFclones had non-stochastic MP of TE differenciation, with no contribution of clone size. Production of MP TRM-like by clones predicted the production of TRM by the same clone, and similarly for TCIRCM and MP TCIRCM. Production of TE was associated with dimunition in both TRM and TCIRCM production.
>
>**Experiment** : Vaccination of two distinct skin site with barcoded cells
>**Results** : There was similarity in clones between the two skin sites, further suggesting non-stochastich differenciation
>
>**Experiment** : Vaccination at a skin site, then in another skin site
>**Results** : There was less TRM in the second site after the first vaccination, which increased following second vaccination. Clones analysis revealed that secondary vaccination-formed TRM ressembled first vaccination-formed TRM and dissresembled TCIRCM. The similarity between second/first vaccination skin TRM was comparable to those of TRM generated by two concurent vaccination.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Following local inflammation, CD8+ T cells develop into tissue-resident memory T cells (TRM). Through combined lineage tracing and single-cell transcriptome analysis, the authors show that the circulating T cell pool harbors TRM precursors that commit to the TRM fate before tissue entry., An increasing body of evidence emphasizes the role of tissue-resident memory T cells (TRM) in the defense against recurring pathogens and malignant neoplasms. However, little is known with regard to the origin of these cells and their kinship to other CD8+ T cell compartments. To address this issue, we followed the antigen-specific progeny of individual naive CD8+ T cells to the T effector (TEFF), T circulating memory (TCIRCM), and TRM pools by lineage-tracing and single-cell transcriptome analysis. We demonstrate that a subset of T cell clones possesses a heightened capacity to form TRM, and that enriched expression of TRM–fate-associated genes is already apparent in the circulating TEFF offspring of such clones. In addition, we demonstrate that the capacity to generate TRM is permanently imprinted at the clonal level, before skin entry. Collectively, these data provide compelling evidence for early stage TRM fate decisions and the existence of committed TRM precursor cells in the circulatory TEFF compartment.,
