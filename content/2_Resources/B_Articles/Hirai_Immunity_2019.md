## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article is the next article from the team behind [[Mohammed_NatImmunol_2016a]], and they focused on the impact of keratinocyte alphaVbeta6/8 activation of TGFb on the TRM establishment int he skin. The use of ITGB6 KO + ITGB8 keratinocyte-specific KO showed loos of CD103+ antigen-specific TRM in the skin, and remaining cell were recirculating. There was a reduction in MP in favor to TE in the spleen, but only with skin and not with systemic infection, and this was not due to difference in priming. This was rescued by constitutive inductible TGFb signaling in CD8 T cells in the ITGB6/8 KO mouse. It was observed that there pertussis toxin sensitive recruitement of T cells in the skin when transfering skin infected, but not systemic infected T cells in a naive mouse. Maintenance of skin-homing recirculating T cells was TGFb dependant. KO mouse had less protection from re-challenge when transfering 14 dpi T cells from WT mouse before. [[TRM Effector]] [[TRM Migration]] [[TRM Persistance]] [[TRM Differenciation]]
> 
>**Experiment** : Infection of ITGB6-/- / ITGB8 keratinocyte conditionnal KO mouse with OVA-VV via skin scarification.
>**Results** : Same viral clearance at 14 dpi in KO and WT mouse. There was less OT-I TRM or B8R-specific in the KO mouse, and most remaining cells were CD103- and CD69-. FTY720 showed that theye were likely recirculating.
>
>**Other Results** : Expansion of B8R+ cells was similar in 14 dpi spleen, but there was a gradual reduction in the KO mouse. This reduction was mostly CD127+KLRG1- MP cells, and increase of TE. Effect was similar but reduced in ITGB6 or ITGB8 single KO, and was absent when infection was systemic suggesting skin-specific effect.
>**Experiment** : Transfer of WT naive or infected mouse T cells in a WT/KO mouse and infection.
>**Results** : Similarly to previous results, there was less MP, suggesting a effecter beyong d14 and not in priming.
>
>**Experiment** : Use of tamoxifen-induced TGFb constitutive signaling specifically in CD8 T cells, monitorable with expression of hNGFR. Isolation of these cells 14 dpi, CFSE labelling and transfer in WT/KO mouse.
>**Results** : There was the same amount MP/TE in B8R+ CD8+ T cells in WT but less MP in the hNGFR+ compared to hNGFR- in KO, suggesting that TGFb signaling rescue the alpha6beta6/alphaVbeta8 defficiency induced MP loss.
>
>**Other Results** : Using the CXCR3/CX3CR1 strategy for Tcm, Tpm and Tem showed that Tcm and Tpm were reduced in the KO mouse. Using E/P-selectin binding cell strategy, both were reduced in KO mouse, suggesting less skin-homing cells.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of skin-infected or systemic-infected CD8 T cell into naive mouse with or with pertussis toxin treatment
>**Results** : In the absence of infection, there was recruitment of CD8 T cells from skin but not systemic infected mouse into the skin, and pertsussi toxin treatment abrogated this observation. Number of skin MP was not altered in WT but decrease in skin MP in KO mouse was restored when pre-treating with pertussis toxin. This suggest that maintenance of skin-homing cells require alphaVbeta6/8 activated TGfb.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of OT-I and VV-OVA infection, then retrieve of 14 dpi splenic T cells and transfer in WT/KO mouse, and infection with VV-OVA 14 days after when memory is formed.
>**Results** : WT mouse had more protection, less viral load. KO was about the same to no transfer mouse. There was less TRM in KO mouse.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Regulated activation of the cytokine TGFβ by integrins αvβ6 and αvβ8 expressed on keratinocytes is required for residence of epidermal-resident memory T cells, but whether skin-derived signals also affect recirculating memory cells in the skin remains unclear. Here, we show that after resolution of skin vaccinia virus (VV) infection, antigen-specific circulating memory CD8+ T cells migrated into skin. In mice lacking αvβ6 and αvβ8 integrins (Itgb6−/−Itgb8fl/fl-K14-cre), the absence of epidermal activated TGFβ resulted in a gradual loss of E- or P-selectin-binding central and peripheral memory populations, that was rescued when skin entry was inhibited. Skin recirculating memory cells were required for optimal host defense against skin VV infection. These data demonstrate that skin migration can persist after resolution of local skin infection and that the cytokine environment within this nonlymphoid tissue shapes the differentiation state and persistence of the central and peripheral memory T cell pool., Whether circulating memory CD8+ T cells enter uninflamed skin remains unclear. Hirai et al. show that after resolution of skin Vaccinia Virus infection, circulating CD8+ memory T cells migrate through the skin and require the cytokine TGFβ for their persistence. These findings demonstrate that the peripheral tissue environment shapes central and peripheral memory T cell pools.,
