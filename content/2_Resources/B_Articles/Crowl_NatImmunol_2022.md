> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article used RNA/scRNA/ATAC seq in TRM from various tissue to assess for TRM heterogeneity. It found that IEL have the highest heterogeneity and differentiation. It also found that tissue dissociation induce the expression of some genes that were associated with TRM signatures. TGFb was higher in IEL/SG, and TGFbRII inducible KO showed dependence in these tissu sites. ATAC seq show correlation with RNAseq, and showed that CD62L, CXCR4 and CCR9 are epigenetically regulated. Some TF were assessed, including BLIMP1 which KO resulted in reduction of KO over WT in IEL. HIC1 shRNA/OE showed complexe results that changed depending on LCMV/LM infection. Globally, it seemed like HIC1 KO were underepresented without affecting memory populations in LCMV infection. OE were overrepresented in most tissue and there was more TRM in IEL at 7 but not 20 dpi. Mechanistically, HIC1 KO resulted in lower P2RX7 expression, potentially having an effect on TRM differentiation. >**Experiment** : scRNAseq of 30-40 dpi transfered P14 T cells in mouse subsequently infected with LCMV Arm from various organs. TRM were distinguished by intravascular ab injection
>**Results** : CD69 and CD103 expression was different between organs as well as P14 proportion. IEL were the most transcriptionally different and had the lowest EOMES/TCF1/KLF2/S1PR1/CD62L expression. It expressed the most GZMA/GMZB and CD127, with lower CD122 suggesting IL15 independance and highest differentiation. + CCR9 in IEL and CXCR4 in fat + CKI highest in IEL
>
>**Experiment** : Comparison of 37°C collagenase dissociated transcriptome and 4°C CAP/DTE dissociation
>**Results** : Some gene showed to be part of TRM signature were specifically upregulated by 37°C collagenase dissociation.
>
>**Other Results** : scRNAseq showed clusters; There is liver cluster with CD127/TCF1/IFNg and another with KLRG1/GZMA/GZMB. IEL/kidney/SG had intra-tissu heterogeneity with TCF1/IL18R1/LY6C2. New heterogeneity-related genes included ODC1, RGS1 and DUSP2. There was negative regulator of TGFb gene expression in SG and IEL. CXCR4 expression in fat. IEL expressed NR4A2/EGR1/CD160. There is IFN stimulated cluster from various clusters.
>
>**Other Results** : There is high TGFb signature in IEL/SG and high CD103 expression.
>**Experiment** : Co-transfer of WT/TGFbRII KO P14 T cells in mouse then infection with LCMV, and deletion of TGFbRII by tamoxifen treatment at 14-18 dpi.
>**Results** : There was a specific reduction of KO cells only in SG and IEL, and KO cells had less CD103 expression in these tissue, suggesting a role for TGFbRII in TRM maintenance in these tissues.
>
>**Experiment** : ATAC-seq of P14 isolated from similar context
>**Results** : IEL and spleen were the most different. After DAR association with nearest gene and comparison with DEG, most of them were in the same direction.  CD62L, CD103 and CCR9 were epigenetically regulated. In FC, there was downregulation of EOMES/TBET/TCF1 in TRM. Analyses of TF motif enrichment with PageRank importance showed highest association of residency TF in IEL (BLIMP1) and repression of KLF2, EOMES, TCF1. RUNX3 was high in IEL/kidney/SG/Spleen. NR4A1 and JUN was higher in TRM from various tissu to circulating cells. AHR and HIC1 had the strongest score in gut-specific.
>**Other Results** : Co-transfer of WT/BLIMP1 KO showed reduction of KO cells in P14 T cells from IEL compared to kidney, suggesting a role of BLIMP1 in IEL TRM at 60 dpi.
>
>**Experiment** : Co-transfer of WT/HIC1 shRNA-transduced P14 T cells in a mouse subsenquently infected with LCMV
>**Results** : There was an increase in HIC1 KO over WT in SG at 7 dpi, but a decrase in kidney/SI at 20-21 dpi. There was no change in TRM proportion in kidney/SI with LCMV infection but there was reduction with LM-gp33 with no changes in KLRG1/CD127 populations.
>**Experiment** : Co-transfer of WT/HIC1 OE-transduced P14 T cells in a mouse subsenquently infected with LCMV
>**Results** : There was more HIC1 OE/WT in IEL than spleen at 7 dpi. At 20 dpi, there was more HIC1 OE than WT in spleen, blood, kidney, IEL but less in SG. There was more TRM in IEL in HIC1 OE at 7 dpi, but not at 20 dpi.
>**Other Results** : Transcriptomic of HIC1 OE vs WT showed higher P2RX7 expression in HIC1 OE, validated at the protein level.
>**Other Results** : Virus titers were lower in mouse receiving HIC1 OE vs WT at 3 dpi in spleen.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Tissue-resident memory T cells (TRM cells) provide protective immunity, but the contributions of specific tissue environments to TRM cell differentiation and homeostasis are not well understood. In the present study, the diversity of gene expression and genome accessibility by mouse CD8+ TRM cells from distinct organs that responded to viral infection revealed both shared and tissue-specific transcriptional and epigenetic signatures. TRM cells in the intestine and salivary glands expressed transforming growth factor (TGF)-β-induced genes and were maintained by ongoing TGF-β signaling, whereas those in the fat, kidney and liver were not. Constructing transcriptional–regulatory networks identified the transcriptional repressor Hic1 as a critical regulator of TRM cell differentiation in the small intestine and showed that Hic1 overexpression enhanced TRM cell differentiation and protection from infection. Provision of a framework for understanding how CD8+ TRM cells adapt to distinct tissue environments, and identification of tissue-specific transcriptional regulators mediating these adaptations, inform strategies to boost protective memory responses at sites most vulnerable to infection.
