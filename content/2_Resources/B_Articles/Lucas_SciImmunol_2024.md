## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article describe that sorted LLEC (KLRG1+CX3CR1+) can still migrate and persist in NLT, most notably lung, and differentiate into TRM. When using systemic LCMV infection and transfer of P14 TCM/TEM/LLEC, it was observed that LLEC do not differentiate into blood TCM/TEM but that they could migrate into lung parenchyma with a low proliferation KLRG1+ phenotype. When using localized IAV-gp33 infection, there was the same amount of lung cells that originated from LLEC and TCM with same Ki67+. CX3CR1+ were not able to replicate this. LLEC depended on endothelial MHC-I expression but not TCM. Finally, LLEC were better than TEM/TCM at persisting in the tissue and they changed phenotype to CD69+CD103+KLRG1-CX3CR1-. [[TRM Caracteristics]] [[TRM Caracteristics]] [[TRM Precursors]]
> 
>**Experiment** : LCMV and gp33-influenza infection in mouse with P14 T cells
>**Results** : There was KLRG1+CX3CR1+ LLECs in the spleen red pulp/blood and in the lung vasculature (when influenza), and they do not express CD69/CD103.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of P14 TCM, TEM or KLRG1+CX3CR1+CD62L- LLEC in a naive mouse and infection with LCMV Arm
>**Results** : TCM and TEM gave rise to LLEC, and LLEC stayed KLRG1+CX3CR1+ without conversion to TCM or TEM in the blood. There was also cells in spleen/NLT, including LLEC in the tissue parenchyma. LLEC had low proliferation, and kept KLRG1 once in the tissue.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of P14 TCM, TEM or KLRG1+CX3CR1+CD62L- LLEC in a naive mouse and infection with local infection IAV-gp33
>**Results** : There was the same amount of TCM or LLEC in the lung tissue with similar Ki67+ and protective capacities. Using CX3CR1 rapporter cells, there was about 5% of these cells that entered the lung after IAV infection.
>
>**Experiment** : Co-transfer of P14 LLEC/TCM in mouse and infection with IAV-gp33
>**Results** : There was more LLEC in the lung than TCM.
>**Experiment** : Same experiment, but with tamoxifen-inducable MHC-I KO in endothelial cells
>**Results** : LLEC recruitement in the lung was dependant on MHC-I on endothelial cells, but not TCM.
>
>**Experiment** : Study of tissue persistance
>**Results** : TCM/TEM did not persist after infection resolution. LLEC-derived cells persisted and down-regulated KLRG1/CX3CR1 and up-regulated CD69/CD103
>
>**Experiment** : Confirmation with CITE-seq of cells from kidney, SI, LN and spleen in the same context with no additional results.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > KLRG1+ CD8 T cells persist for months after clearance of acute infections and maintain high levels of effector molecules, contributing protective immunity against systemic pathogens. Upon secondary infection, these long-lived effector cells (LLECs) are incapable of forming other circulating KLRG1− memory subsets such as central and effector memory T cells. Thus, KLRG1+ memory T cells are frequently referred to as a terminally differentiated population that is relatively short lived. Here, we show that after viral infection of mice, effector cells derived from LLECs rapidly enter nonlymphoid tissues and reduce pathogen burden but are largely dependent on receiving antigen cues from vascular endothelial cells. Single-cell RNA sequencing reveals that secondary memory cells in nonlymphoid tissues arising from either KLRG1+ or KLRG1− memory precursors develop a similar resident memory transcriptional signature. Thus, although LLECs cannot differentiate into other circulating memory populations, they still retain the flexibility to enter tissues and establish residency.
