## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article studied effector/memory T cells differentiation using scRNAseq, and discover early changes in division 1 cells. These celles separated into two distinct transcriptomes with resemblance to TE and memory T cells. Some of the genes had rapid high expression and repression in effector-like cells, suggesting that they may be epigenetic regulators. EZH2 was one of them, and it's KO resulted in lower total CD8+ T cells, lower cytokine secretion and more apoptosis in effector vs memory T cells. H3K27me3 ChIPseq revealde more peaks correlated with RNA repression in effector T cells, and there was EZH2 targets that were less expressed including memory genes. [[Effector_Memory cell formation]] [[TRM Epigenetic]]
> 
>**Experiment** : Transfer of P14 T cells into mouse, then infection with LCMV Arm. Retrieval of P14 T cells at 2, 4, 7 dpi CD44+ enriched spleen+ TCM/TEM/Tnaive sorted 42 dpi. There also was division 1 (CFSE peak sorted) cells. + scRNAseq
>**Results** : Division 1 cells had two distinct transcriptome resembling effector or naive/Tcm. Div1TE had terminal effector pathways, and Div1mem had CD127, S1PR1 and KLF2 expression. However TCF1/BACH2, LEF1 expression was similar and EOMES/ID3/FOXO1 was higher in Div1TE. A model to predict fate was trained and accurately labeled intermediary timepoint cells.
>**Other Results** : Shared DEG between Div1TE vs Div1mem and TE vs mem had 89 genes. Some of the genes in TE fate were quickly expressed and had rapid decline; this could be due to epigenetics actors including EZH2 and other PRC2 complex protein EED, SUZ12, SET with validation at the protein level in CD25hiCD62Llow and CD25loCD62Lhi cells.
>
>**Experiment** : T cell conditionnal KO of EZH2, and co-trasnfer of KO/WT
>**Results** : KO cells were numerously less present at 5/8 dpi, and secreted less cytokines. Effector cells undergo apoptosis preferentially compared to memory T cells.
>
>**Experiment** : ChIP-seq of H3K27me3 peaks in naive, TE and memory (CD44hi)
>**Results** : TE had more H3K27me3 peaks with anticorrelation in RNA expression. EZH2 targets were involved in pathways including transcriptional regulation, cytoskeletal protein binding, phosphoinositide binding, Wnt receptor signaling, and apoptosis.
>**Other Results** : Analysis of EZH2 target which expression decrease in TE differentiation; TCF1, EOMES, SMAD2, BCAT1, KLF2, OPA1, FOXO1, TCF1, ID3, EOMES, CD127, LEF1, BCL2, 
>**Other Results** : Effector genes BATF/IRF4/TBET/CD25/AKT1/HIF1A/MYC had similar gene expression pattern to EZH2, and lower expression in memory T cells.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > During microbial infection, responding CD8+ T lymphocytes differentiate into heterogeneous subsets that together provide immediate and durable protection. To elucidate the dynamic transcriptional changes that underlie this process, we applied a single-cell RNA sequencing approach and analyzed individual CD8+ T lymphocytes sequentially throughout the course of a viral infection in vivo. Our analyses revealed a striking transcriptional divergence among cells that had undergone their first division and identified previously unknown molecular determinants controlling CD8+ T lymphocyte fate specification. These findings suggest a model of terminal effector cell differentiation initiated by an early burst of transcriptional activity and subsequently refined by epigenetic silencing of transcripts associated with memory lymphocytes, highlighting the power and necessity of single-cell approaches.
