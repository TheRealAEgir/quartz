## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article describe an activation of CD103 expressing naive T cells by pDC/mDC through IFNb and IL12. [[1.1 Différenciation des lymphocytes T en lymphocyte T effecteur ou mémoire]]
> 
>**Experiment** : Assessement of CD8 T cells from LN
>**Results** : There was a CD103neg that had a MP phenotype, with memory proteins without activation markers, as well as basal proliferation and IFNg production. Most naive T cells expressed CD103, but memory and VM lacked CD103. This was visible using Ly6C/CXCR3 classification, where DN are CD103-expressing naive, DP are VM and Ly6C SP are intermediate.
>**Other Results** : CD103 expression decreased when DN are transfered in another mouse, with or without cognate antigen.
>**Other Results** : CD103neg population is increased in IL15 OE mouse, but decreased in IL15 KO mouse.
>
>**Experiment** : Pharmacological inhibition of STAT5 and Akt
>**Results** : Only combinaison resulted in decrease of BCL2 and impaired CD103 downregulation. In a mouse with constitutive STAT5 signaling, naive T cells have less CD103 expression.
>
>**Experiment** : CD103 KO mouse
>**Results** : No impact on most CD8+ T cells populations expect for the Ly6C+CXCR3- population which was decreased, suggesting a role for CD103 in establishing this population. In co-transfer of WT/CD103 KO experiment, there is less Ly6C+CXCR3- T cells in LN/spleen in CD103 KO population, without affecting BCL2/KI67.
>
>**Other Results** : E-cadherin was not expressed on most pLN cells tested except mDC/pDC but not resident DC. This was validated in CITEseq.
>**Experiment** : E cadherin KO in DC
>**Results** : DP and DN were not affected, but there was less Ly6C+CXCR3- without affecting CD103 expression. Transfer of DN in WT E cad-DC KO mouse resulted in similar reduction in the KO mouse.
>**Experiment** : Bulk RNAseq of CD103 KO T cells
>**Results** : There was a loss of STAT1 and IFN type I pathways, which were validated by STAT1 KO, IFNAR1 KO and *in vitro* IFNb/IFNg, all affecting the Ly6C+CXCR3- population. IFNb administration in Ecad-DC KO mouse reversed the results, and CpG activated pDC/supernatent differenciated DN into SP. IFNAR1 KO T cell did not, suggesting that IFNb is the major responsible.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of OT-I into WT/Ecad DC KO mouse
>**Results** : Increase of tumor burden in KO, and decrease of IFNg/GZMA secretion by TILs. CD103KO OT-I had similar results and lower diferenciation into SP.
>**Experiment** : Co transfer of CD103 KO vs OE in mouse WT or Ecad-DC KO.
>**Results** : CD103 KO were impaired in SP formation, and CD103 OE was increase in WT mouse.
>
>**Experiment** : Bulk RNAseq of WT vs CD103 OE T cells
>**Results** : Proliferation in OE and apoptosis in WT, without difference in BrdU incorporation but lower active caspase 3 in OE. BCL2/BCLXL and ID2 were higher in DP.
>
>**Other Results** : In coculture model, mDC but not pDC drive DN toward SP and DP populations.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Integrin CD103 binds to E-cadherin, a cell adhesion molecule predominantly expressed on epithelial cells, thus mediating the tissue-residency of CD103+ T cells in barrier sites. Importantly, circulating naïve CD8+ T cells also express large amounts of CD103, but whether CD103 contributes to CD8+ T cell immunity beyond its role in cell adhesion is unclear. Here, we report that CD103 expression in naïve CD8+ T cells facilitates their engagement with E-cadherin-expressing cells, promoting their acquisition of memory phenotype and effector function. Notably, dendritic cell (DC) subsets expressing E-cadherin and producing type I interferons and IL-12 were responsible for this process. As a corollary, the DC-specific loss of E-cadherin resulted in diminished effector CD8+ T cell differentiation and increased tumor susceptibility, while the forced expression of CD103 enhanced the effector functions and anti-tumor activity of CD8+ T cells, revealing a regulatory role for CD103 in cytotoxic T cell immunity., Integrin CD103 mediates memory T cell tissue-residency by binding E-cadherin-expressing epithelial cells, but why CD103 is also expressed on circulating naïve CD8+ T cells has been unclear. Li et al. report that CD103 facilitates the interaction of naïve CD8+ T cells with E-cadherin-expressing dendritic cells that in turn provide cytokine-mediated cues to promote their differentiation into memory-phenotype effector T cells.,
