## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article studied DC and showed that mDC in LN epigenetically predispose naive T cells to differenciate into TRM through ITGAV activated TGFb. By using a ITGAV DC conditionnal KO mouse, it was observed that under homeostatic condition, but not when antigen was present, that there was less CD69+CD103+CD8+ epidermis skin TRM without impacting CD4, dermis or CD69/CD103 expression on CD4/DC. The generated TRM in KO mouse had less residency stability and were more depleted with intravascular antibody injection. Naive T cells from KO mice had a distinct epigenetic profile, with some genes corresponding to TRM such as S1PR5 accessibility upregulation, or ITGAE/AHR/CCR8/RUNX3 downregulation. CD103 expression was not intrisically impaired, and seemed to appear in LN under normal conditions. Finally, mDC seemed more important than other DCs, and this effect was MHC-i interaction dependant. [[TRM Differenciation]] [[TRM Precursors]] [[TRM Epigenetic]] [[TRM Differenciation]]
> 
>**Experiment** : Use of a conditionnal KO of alphaV ITGAV protein in DC (CD11c) to deplete DC activation of TGFb.
>**Results** : There was a small expansion of CD4+ and cytokine production increase, but no sign of inflammation. It was reported that ITGB8 DC KO lead to colitis due to Treg/Th17 absence. There was less CD8 in skin epidermis which expressed less CD69 and CD103, without impacting dermis CD8/CD4 nor CD69/CD103 expression in CD4/DC. This was observed at homeostasis and was increased with antigen free inflammation.
>**Other Results** : When adding an antigen in inflammated skin, there was the same amount of TRM in WT/KO mouse. WT/KO transferred naive T cells had similar TRM differentiation capacities with DNA plasmid vaccination.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of naive T cells from WT/KO mouse in mouse, and vaccination 3 weeks after
>**Results** : 4 weeks after immunization, there was less TRM in KO mouse. When transfering the cells directly prior to vaccination, there was tenfold less TRM in KO mouse.
>
>**Experiment** : Use of Tcirc depleting antibody
>**Results** : Similar effect of the treatment in both mice, but more pronounced in KO mouse. After treatment, KO mouse was less protected against re-challenge. This suggested that among remaining TRM, these are less resident in KO mouse.
>
>**Experiment** : ATACseq of naive T cells from WT/KO mouse
>**Results** : Profile of T cells from WT seemed more "ready" to be expressed with DAR at TSS. In KO, there was enrichment of activation TF and IRF. In WT, there was enrichment of KLF, ZFP and RUNX (with RUNX3). Other genes included ITGAE/AHR/CCR8 in WT and S1PR5 in KO
>
>**Other Results** : There was no difference in CD103 expression in thymocytes, suggesting that there exist alternative way of TGFb activation in this organ.
>**Other Results** : CD103 expression was possible on T cells from KO mouse, when *in vitro* TGFb stimulation or transfer into WT mouse. Transfer of T cells froms WT mouse into KO mouse resulted in no CD103 expression.
>**Experiment** : FTY720 to trap CD103hi thymocytes in the thymus
>**Results** : CD103hi cells did not came from thymus
>**Experiment** : Use of LTa KO mouse which don't have LN
>**Results** : Naive T cells from WT mouse had similar CD103 expression to ITGAV KO, and could express CD103 upon *in vitro* TGFb treatment.
>These results suggested no intrisic impairment of T cells to express CD103
>
>**Experiment** : Infection iv with LM-OVA and skin DNFB injection in LTA KO mouse
>**Results** : Comparable CD103 expression with ITGAV KO mice skin CD8 T cells
>**Experiment** : Transfer of naive T cells from ITGAV KO mouse into WT mouse, and FTY720 to block these cells into LN
>**Results** : There was high upregulation of CD103 from transfered naive T cells.
>
>**Experiment** : CCR7 KO mouse in which mDC are absent in spleen/LN 
>**Results** : CD103 expression was reduced in naive T cells. Similar observation in naive T cells from WT mouse transfered into CCR7 KO mouse.
>
>**Experiment** : Bone marrow chimera of WT/B2M KO to remove MHC-I dependent interactions
>**Results** : TRM differentiation was impaired, suggesting that DC do not secrete an active pool of TGFb readily accessible for naive T cells without MHC-I interaction.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Epithelial resident memory T (eTRM) cells serve as sentinels in barrier tissues to guard against previously encountered pathogens. How eTRM cells are generated has significant implications for efforts to elicit their formation through vaccination or prevent it in autoimmune disease. Here we show that during immune homeostasis, the cytokine TGF-β epigenetically conditions resting naive CD8+ T cells and prepares them for the formation of eTRM cells in a mouse model of skin vaccination. Naive T cell conditioning occurs in lymph nodes (LNs), but not in the spleen, through MHC I-dependent interactions with peripheral tissue-derived migratory dendritic cells (DCs) and depends on DC-expression of TGF-β-activating αV-integrins. Thus, the pre-immune T cell repertoire is actively conditioned for a specialized memory differentiation fate through signals restricted to LNs., Naive T cells are trained in lymph nodes in their resting state to stand guard in tissues as local memory cells in the future.
