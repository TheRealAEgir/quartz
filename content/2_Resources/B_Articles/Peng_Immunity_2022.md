## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article showed that ICOS signalling is important is the TRM generation. The ICOS signalling is not important during priming, but rather during activation in NLT. ICOSLG seem to be produced by radioresistant non-hematopoeitic cells. Finally, the transcriptome of TRM formed without ICOS signaling is very similar, and removing ICOS-PI3K signalling impact the number of TRM [[TRM Phenotype]] [[TRM Differenciation]] [[TRM Therapies]]
> 
>**Experiment** : Co-transfer of WT/ICOS KO P14 T cells into mice, then LCMV Arm infection. (verified in LM-gp33 and PR8-gp33, and with BM mixing, and with ICOSLG Ko mouse)
>**Results** : At memory timepoint: no change in blood/SLO and TCM/TEM/LLEC proportions, but KO are decreased in IEL, LP, kidney and SG. KO cells in NLT still displayed a TRM phenotype. Initial entry at effector timepoint was not affected, suggesting an effect in TRM phenotype acquiring.
>**Other Results** : The advantage of WT cells over ICOS cells are lost in a ICOSLG KO mouse, which shows impaired TRM generation in both WT and ICOS KO. The use of a ICOSLG blocking antibody showed that only early treatment, but not treatment at memory timepoint, impair TRM generation. ICOS OE lead to enhanced TRM generation in IEL and kidney.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of WT P14 T cells from WT/ICOSLG mouse into mouse with the opposite genotype at 4.5 dpi, which aim to separate SLO primed T cells from NLT activation
>**Results** : There was no advantage of pre 4.5 dpi (during priming) ICOS signaling, but removing ICOS signaling in NLT impaired TRM formation. True even in naive secondary host without infection, suggesting ICOS signaling is not inflammation dependant.
>
>**Experiment** : To determine which cell produce ICOSLG, irradiation of mouse.
>**Results** : There was no effect of radiation, suggesting that ICOSLG is produced by radioresistant, probably non-hematopoetic cells. Reconstitution of irradiated mouse with BM did not rescue the observation, suggesting that hematopoeitic cells are not sufficient.
>
>**Experiment** : scRNAseq for co-transfer WT/ICOS T cells
>**Results** : KO and WT was very similar, but KO had less TRM cells. One of the result was KLF2 expression increase in ICOS KO T cells
>**Other Results** : Co-transfer of WT/ICOS KO KLF2-GFP rapporter T cells : KO had more expression of KLF2, mostly in NLT.
>
>**Experiment** : Use of ICOSyf-yf (ICOS PI3K signalisation incompetent)
>**Results** : Less TRM when no ICOS signalling.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Elevated gene expression of the costimulatory receptor Icos is a hallmark of CD8+ tissue-resident memory (Trm) T cells. Here, we examined the contribution of ICOS in Trm cell differentiation. Upon transfer into WT mice, Icos−/− CD8+ T cells exhibited defective Trm generation but produced recirculating memory populations normally. ICOS deficiency or ICOS-L blockade compromised establishment of CD8+ Trm cells but not their maintenance. ICOS ligation during CD8+ T cell priming did not determine Trm induction; rather, effector CD8+ T cells showed reduced Trm differentiation after seeding into Icosl−/− mice. IcosYF/YF CD8+ T cells were compromised in Trm generation, indicating a critical role for PI3K signaling. Modest transcriptional changes in the few Icos−/− Trm cells suggest that ICOS-PI3K signaling primarily enhances the efficiency of CD8+ T cell tissue residency. Thus, local ICOS signaling promotes production of Trm cells, providing insight into the contribution of costimulatory signals in the generation of tissue-resident populations.
