## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%

>
>**Summary** : This article used HSV infection in mice which generated TRM in skin, and described a CD103+BCL2+CD69+ TRM population. These cells depend on CD69 for tissue entry and CD103 at latter time point, as well as chemokine receptor (most probably CXCR3). Their precursor do not express KLRG1. CCR7 KO induced more TRM cells. These cells depends on TGRb and IL-15, as TGFbR2-/- or IL-15-/- generate less CD103 TRM. Transcriptionnal analysis revealed some key features, including CCR9 for gut TRM, CCR8 for skin TRM, AHR for both, among other.
>
>**Model** : Mice infection with HSV and co-transfert of gBT-I specific T cells.
>**Results** : This model shows rapid ~7 days induction of TRM in the skin that expressed CD103, BCL2 and CD69, particularly in the epidermis.
>
>**Experiment** : Co-transfert of WT and CD69-/- or CD103-/- into an HSV infected mice
>**Results** : Deletion of CD69 or CD103 resulted in a loss or cells in the skin epidermis. CD69 has a early effect while CD103 has a much later effect, and CD103 was dispensable for entry of T cells. Early skin T cells were KLRG1+CD103- while latter was KLRG1-CD103+
>
>**Experiment** : Transfert of KLRG1+- sorted gBT-I isolated from spleen.
>**Results** : Only KLRG1- subset generated CD103+ TRM in the skin.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfert of *in vitro* HSV peptide activated gBT-I cells into mice
>**Results** : Transferred T cells migrated into epidermis.
>**Model** : *in vitro* treatment of the activated gBT-I cells with pertussis toxin, which blocks chemokine-driven migration.
>**Results** : Treatment blocked both epidermis infiltration and CD103 expression. *In vitro* treatment of those cells showed upregulation of CD103, meaning that the pertussis toxin treatment do not block the expression of CD103.
>
>**Experiment** : KLRG1- and KLRG1+ in transwell assay toward CXCL9/CXCL10
>**Results** : KLRG1- which expressed more CXCR3 had higher migration.
>**Experiment** : Intradermal injection of CXCR3-/-
>**Results** : Those cells generated fewer CD103+ TRM in the epidermis.
> %% end Notes %%
> **Experiment** : CCR7-/- T cells (transfert ?)
> **Results** : Those cells generated more TRM.
>
>**Experiment** : intradermal injection of *in vitro* peptide activated OT-I WT or TGFBR2-/-
>**Results** : KO expressed less CD103 and where lost from the skin. Same with co-transfert then infection with HSV-ovalbumin.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfert of gBT-I primed from a naive mice into WT or IL15-/- mice.
>**Results** : IL15-/- mice showed reduced TRM and reduced CD103 and BCL2 expression.
>
>**Experiment** : Microarray transcriptome analysis of Tcirc, Trm and other resident population
>**Results** : TRM are a transcriptionnaly distinct population. CCR9 (gut), CCR8 (skin), AHR (gut and skin), S1PR1 downregulation (all)
