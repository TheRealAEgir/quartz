## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article described a TCF1+PD1+ T cell population that is able to expand and give rise to both TCF1+ and TCF1- T cells in tumors. The use of TCF1 KO mouse in a tumor vaccination model showed that depletion of this TF lead to less T cells in tumor/tdLN/spleen. Kinetic showed acquisition of PD1 and then loss of TCF1, and only antigen-experienced had PD1 expression. TFC1+ were localized closely to CD31+ endothelial cells. Inducible depletion of TCF1+ T cells lead to decrease in T cell number and tumor growth control in a Tcirc independent manner. Similar results were found with ICB (anti PD1 anti CTLA4). Comparison of TCF1+ and TCF1- showed that TCF1+ had memory and stem-like capacities, proliferation, less apoptosis, IL2 producion, less Tbet and more EOMES, LAG3/PD1/CTLA4 expression. TCF1 was essential to the observation, and ChIP show repression of genes such as granzymes, KLGR1, ID2. Finally, this was validated in human melanoma, where patient had TCF1+PD1+ in blood and tumor were it localized within TLS structures. These cells were more numerous in ICB treated patients, and combined TCF1/PD1 was associated with prognosis in T cell high TCGA analysis. [[Tpex]]
> 
>**Experiment** : Transfer of WT of TCF1 KO P14 T cells and then subcutaneous inoculation of B16-gp33 tumour cells. Then, gp33 vaccination with poly(I:C) adjuvant
>**Results** : Similar growth rate. Addition of vaccination lead to transient tumour regression, and recurrence was quicker and earlier in TCF1 KO. There was 10 fold less P14 T cells in the tumour and 3 fold in the tdLN at endpoint but similar concentration at intermediate timepoint.
>**Other Results** : In WT mouse, naive P14 T cells expressed TCF1, then PD1 in middle sized tumour and lose TCF1 expression in large tumours. When adding vaccination, there was expansion of TCF1+PD1+ TILs and these remained at endpoint. Both TCF1- and TCF+ are resident by intravascular staining.
>**Other Results** : There was TCF1+PD1+ P14 and TCF1+PD1- T cells in the tdLN. There was no PD1 expression in distant LN or spleen event if P14 were antigen-experienced (CD44+). TCF1+PD1+ were also detected in other tumour settings.
>
>**Other Results** : While P14 were scattered throughout the tumor, TCF1+ were prefenrentially enriched in the vicinity of CD31+ endothelial structures and TCF1- in the tumor parenchyma. They were not enriched in TLS-like structures.
>
>**Experiment** : TCF1(GFP-DTR) model where TCF1+ T cells are fluorescent and are depleted after DT treatment.
>**Results** : (validation of the model). DT treatment in tumor-bearing mouse then vaccination resulted in absence of GFP or TCF1+ TILs, tdLN or spleen. There was 2.5 fold less TCF1- P14 TILs, dLN and spleen (5-6 fold). This suggested that TCF1+ was important for TCF1- population and sustain it. Also, tumor progressed only when TCF1+ were depleted.
>
>**Experiment** : To study tumor-resident vs circulating TCF1+ T cells wuth FTY720
>**Results** : Tumor-resident TCF1+ were important to the generation of TCF1-PD1+ population, and TCF1+PD1+ was maintained when TCF1+ were not depleted.
>
>**Experiment** : Same TCF1+ depletion and FTY720 treatment, but with ICB (anti-PD1+anti-CTLA4)
>**Results** : ICB increased TCF1+ and TCF1- TILs by 10 fold in WT, but it was reduced when TCF1+ were depleted. FTY720 treatement did not impacted tumor control, but it was lost when TCF1+ were depleted.
>
>**Experiment** : Transcriptomic analyses of TCF1+ and TCF1-
>**Results** : TCF1 ressembled memory/MP while keeping stem-like features and did not expressed TRM genes (BLIMP1/HOBIT/RUNX3). They did not share SLEC transcroptome. They did share with Tfh.
>**Other Results** : TCF1+ has IL2, KI67 and proliferation and less apoptosis than TCF1- and had the potential to expand upon transfer into TCF1+ and TCF1-. They expressed less Tbet and more EOMES. They had expression of LAG3/PD1/CTLA4.
>
>**Other Results** : TCF1 expression was essential to the effect, as TCF1-GFP+ were not able to recapitulate the results.
>
>**Experiment** : ChIPseq of TCF1
>**Results** : TCF1 repressed granzymes, ID2, KLRG1
>
>**Experiment** : Validation in melanoma patient blood
>**Results** : There was TCF1+/- PD1+ cells in the patient blood and tumors. TCF1- were localized in the tumor stroma, and some TLS contained TCF1+. There was higher TCF1+PD1+ in patient with ICB treatment. TCGA analysis on T cell high patient was prognosis when combined PD1/TCF1.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Checkpoint blockade mediates a proliferative response of tumor-infiltrating CD8+ T lymphocytes (TILs). The origin of this response has remained elusive because chronic activation promotes terminal differentiation or exhaustion of tumor-specific T cells. Here we identified a subset of tumor-reactive TILs bearing hallmarks of exhausted cells and central memory cells, including expression of the checkpoint protein PD-1 and the transcription factor Tcf1. Tcf1+PD-1+ TILs mediated the proliferative response to immunotherapy, generating both Tcf1+PD-1+ and differentiated Tcf1−PD-1+ cells. Ablation of Tcf1+PD-1+ TILs restricted responses to immunotherapy. Tcf1 was not required for the generation of Tcf1+PD-1+ TILs but was essential for the stem-like functions of these cells. Human TCF1+PD-1+ cells were detected among tumor-reactive CD8+ T cells in the blood of melanoma patients and among TILs of primary melanomas. Thus, immune checkpoint blockade relies not on reversal of T cell exhaustion programs, but on the proliferation of a stem-like TIL subset.
