## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article used P2RX7 KO P14 T cells in LCMV mouse to found a role for P2RX7 in memory T cells formation through TGFb responsivness and mitchondrial activity. The removal of P2RX7 lead to decrease in resident and circulating T cells. In the gut, eATP may be produced by microbiota as antibiotic depletion has an impact on TRM cells formation only in WT cells. Parabiosis experiment showed low repopulation of P2RX7 KO cells, which decrease over time and which TRM had low tissue rentention. This seems due to TGFb responsivness, as P2RX7 KO cells had lower expression of TGFb response genes, and TGFb treatment had reduced effect in KO cells. P2RX7 agonist/antagonist had the exppected results in TGFb responsive CD103 expression. This was due to Ca2+ signaling, as blockade of calcineurin removed the effect. Forced TGFbRII expression partially recovered the observed effect, but only in early depletion of P2RX7 and not in late depletion using inducible deletion. [[TRM Phenotype]] [[TRM Differenciation]] [[TRM Therapies]]
> 
>**Experiment** : P14 T cells primed in LCMV Armstrong infected mice.
>**Results** : P2RX7 is expressed by TRM and in some TCM. P2RX7 KO cells failed to establish TRM in SI IEL, FRT, SG, liver and SLOs. Also visible for circulating memory T cells.
>**Experiment** : Antibody depletion of instestinal bacteria, thought to be producing eATP
>**Results** : There was a decrease in WT SI IEL P14 TRM cell number and CD69+CD103+ frequency, but no decrease in P2RX7 KO or cells in SG/SLO, suggesting a partial role for microbiota-derived eATP in gut TRM establishment.
>
>**Other Results** : P2RX7 KO P14 T cells entered SI IEL pool as efficient as WT, but there was a decrease in CD69+CD103+ and CD69-CD103+ populations.
>
>**Experiment** : Parabiosis of co-transfered P2RX7 WT/KO P14 T cells LCMV infected mouse with infected-matched mouse. 
>
>**Results** : There was small amount of P2RX7 KO TRM cells in the parabiont, but the one found in NLT had a TRM phenotype. However, these cells were lost with time, and showed weak retention in the tissue.
>
>**Other Results** : TGFbRII expressin was reduced in P2RX7 KO P14 TRM cells from SI IEL and SI LP. CD103 and TGFbRII, and TGFb sensing pathways gene expression was reduced in spleen 4.5 dpi P 14 T cells, but went back to the same level as WT at 8 dpi. There was no difference in EOMES expression, but a MFI reduction of TBET expression in KO P14 T cells.
>
>**Experiment** : Treatment of P2RX7 WT/KO P14 T cells with TGFb + agonist/antagonist of P2RX7
>**Results** : There was reduction in CD103 upregulation and smad2/3 phosphorylation in KO cells after treatment. Antagonist treatment lead to reduction of CD103 and agonist had the opposite effect, only on WT T cells.
>**Experiment** : Blockade of P2RX7 Ca2+ signaling via calcineurin inhibition
>**Results** : Inhibition of calcineurin lead to reduced TGFb induced CD103 expression only in WT cells.
>
>**Experiment** : P2RX7 KO in KLF2 raporter P14 T cells in mouse
>**Results** : Loss of P2RX7 lead to increase of raporter+ KLF2 T cells in SI IEL 5 dpi.
>
>**Experiment** : Forced TGFbRII expression inP2RX7 KO P14 T cells
>**Results** : Forced TGFbRII expression in P14 T cells lead to increase in TGFb responsivness in vitro and partially rescue the co-expression of CD69+CD103+ T cells in SI IEL. It also rescued CD127+KLRG1- MPEC and SLO TRM which do not depend on CD69/CD103 expression, and this was due to a reduced mitochondrial mass and activity in P2RX7 KO P14 T cells.
>
>**Experiment** : Inducible tamoxifen deletion of P2RX7 in mouse
>**Results** : Late deletion (28-35) of P2RX7 lead to reduction of memory T cells (circulating and resident). Forced expression of TGRbRII rescue was lost when P2RX7 was deleted late but not early.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Tissue-resident memory (Trm) CD8+ T cells mediate protective immunity in barrier tissues, but the cues promoting Trm cell generation are poorly understood. Sensing of extracellular adenosine triphosphate (eATP) by the purinergic receptor P2RX7 is needed for recirculating CD8+ T cell memory, but its role for Trm cells is unclear. Here we showed that P2RX7 supported Trm cell generation by enhancing CD8+ T cell sensing of TGF-β, which was necessary for tissue residency. P2RX7-deficient Trm cells progressively decayed in non-lymphoid tissues and expressed dysregulated Trm-specific markers. P2RX7 was required for efficient re-expression of the receptor TGF-βRII through calcineurin signaling. Forced Tgfbr2 expression rescued P2RX7-deficient Trm cell generation, and TGF-β sensitivity was dictated by P2RX7 agonists and antagonists. Forced Tgfbr2 also rescued P2RX7-deficient Trm cell mitochondrial function. Sustained P2RX7 signaling was required for long-term Trm cell maintenance, indicating that P2RX7 signaling drives induction and CD8+ T cell durability in barrier sites., Borges da Silva et al. report that the extracellular ATP receptor P2RX7 promotes generation of virus-specific tissue-resident memory (Trm) CD8+T cells through induction of the TGF-β signaling pathway. Furthermore, long-term maintenance of Trm cells relies on continuous P2RX7 signaling.,
