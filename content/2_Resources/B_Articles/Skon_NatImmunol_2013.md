## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%
>**Summary** : This study used a KLF2-GFP P14 T cell model to show that NLT resident cell downregulate KLF2. Forced expression of KLF2 resulted in high S1PR1 and SELL expression, and co transfert of forced KLF2 or S1PR1 P14 with WT P14 resulted in reduced number in NLT. KLF2 downregulation in NLT seemed independant of antigen stimulation, shown by transfert in naive mice and with DNFB injection. Finally, KLF2 downregulation seemed controlled by PI3K-AKT through various cytokines, including IL12/TGFb/IL33/TNF/Type I IFN and to various degree IL6 and IL18, while IL15 promote it's expression.
>[[TRM Phenotype]] [[TRM Differenciation]]
>
>**Experiment** :
>**Model** : Use of a KLF2-GFP functional protein mice
>**Results** : KLF2-GFP was observed in Tcm and Tem, despite the fact that these are CD62L+ and - and KLF2 control SELL expression.
>**Model** : The KLF2-GFP was used in P14 T cell specific for gp33-41 LCMV GP. Anti-CD8 antibody was used to remove Tcirc for highly vascularised organs
>**Results** : P14 cells showed high KLF2 expression in spleen, lymph node and blood, but low expression in instestine, salivary gland, kidney and brain (which also showed high CD69). There also was decreased Klf2, S1pr1 and Sell transcript, suggesting that the KLF2 phenotype do not come from KLF2 protein degradation.
>
>**Experiment** : Transduction of P14 for forced KLF2 expression.
>**Result** : These cell increased S1pr1 and Sell mRNA and decreased CD69 expression (although not in mRNA), suggesting that KLF2 do not regulate CD69 through transcript.
>
>**Experiment** : Kinetics of KLF2 in P14 WT and KLF2-GFP P14 in recipient mice and LCMV infection through effector and memory phase.
>**Results** : KLF2 expression dropped in effector cells, and was restored in blod and lymphoid tissues by d5. NLTcells were KLF2low by d8, suggesting that T cells enter the tissue while KLF2hi and then downregulate it.
>
>**Experiment** : Parabiosis of KLF2-GFP P14 mice with mice infected.
>**Results** : Tcirc entering NLT are KLF2int CD69int, which is different from Trm.
>
>**Experiment** : Forced S1PR1 expression and co-transfert with WT in infected mice.
>**Results** : Both cells type were equal in lymphoid tissues, but S1PR1-transduced P14 was considerably lower in NLT. This effect was early, suggesting that removing S1PR1 prevented initial formation. However, they enter at similar rate in an DNFB induced local inflammation. The same effect was observed with KLF2 forced expression.
>
>**Question** : What signal induce the downregulation of KLF2 in NLT ?
>**Experiment** : *In vitro* stimulation of KLF2-GFP P14 cells and transfert in naive mice
>**Results** : KLF2 is lost in NLT without antigen, and kept high in lymphoid tissues.
>**Model** : Nur77-GFP mice, which report TCR stimulation
>**Results** : In a similar model, there is not TCR stimulation, suggesting that the KLF2 observed earlier is indeed TCR independant.
>
>**Experiment** : Culture of *in vivo* activated P14 with TNF, IL-33, TGFb, IL-6, IL-15, IL-12, type I IFN, IL-18
>**Results** : The first three induce KLF2 downregulation, IL-6 is modest, and IL-15 increase the expression (yet combinaison still result in downregulation). Type I IFN and IL-12 as well, IL-18 is modest but high with IL-12.
>TGFb and IL-33  downregulation was dependant of PI3K/AKT.
>**Experiment** : Treatment of mice with a PI3K/AKT inhibitor
>**Results** : Treatment induce an decrease of P14 in NLT, and P14 had higher KLF2-GFP expression, as well as decreased FOXO1.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Cell-mediated immunity critically depends on lymphocyte localization at sites of infection. While some memory T cells recirculate, a distinct lineage (resident memory T cells; TRM) are embedded in non-lymphoid tissues (NLTs) and mediate potent protective immunity. However, the defining transcriptional basis for TRM establishment is unknown. We report that CD8+ TRM cells lacked expression of the transcription factor KLF2 and its target gene S1pr1 (encoding sphingosine 1-phosphate receptor 1). Forced S1PR1 expression prevented establishment of TRM. Cytokines inducing TRM phenotype (including TGF-β, IL-33 and TNF) provoked KLF2 downregulation in a phosphatidylinositol-3-OH kinase (PI(3)K)–Akt-dependent pathway, suggesting environmental regulation. Hence KLF2 and S1PR1 regulation provides a switch, dictating whether CD8+ T cells commit to the recirculating or tissue resident memory populations.
