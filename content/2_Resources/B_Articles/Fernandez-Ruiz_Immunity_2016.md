## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article studied TRM formation in the liver in a malaria vaccination setting, and showed that TRM confer protection to malaria. They observed CD69+/KLRG1-/CD103- resident-verified TRM and CD69-/KLRG1+ TEM in the liver following the vaccine. Two-photon microscopy showed that TRM were ameoboid and travelled slowly in the sinusoid, whereas TEM were round and traveled faster. The use of a vaccination strategy consisting of priming by DC (peptide-Ab anti CLEC9A + CpG) and recruitement to the liver (infection with adenovirus expressing peptide only in hepatocyte) showed higher resident-verified TRM infiltration and protection. Trm had higher positive correlation between cell number and disease control than Tem and higher functionnality/cytotoxicity. Depletion of TRM with anti-CXCR3 lead to the loss of the protection. [[TRM Differenciation]] [[TRM Therapies]] [[TRM Effector]] [[TRM Caracteristics]]
> 
>**Experiment** : GFP+ PbT-I T cells specific for malaria pathogenes in mice vaccinated with radiation-attenuated sporozoites (RAS) and analyses of spleen/liver T cells 31 dpi.
>**Results** : There was KLRG1+-/CXCR6+- populations in the liver, but only KLRG1+CXCR6- in the spleen. The liver population expressed CD69 and was long lived. Transcriptomic showed that liver CD69-KLRG1+ ressembled antigen-specific spleen T cells, suggesting that they were TEM. CD69+KLRG1- ressembled TRM cells but lacked CD103 expression.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of liver T cells in mice. TRM died unless using nanobody s+16a which block ARTC2.2, which sense NAD and ADP-ribosylate P2RX7 causing cell death.
>**Results** : Transfered cells preferentially homed to the liver. Parabiosis experiment showed that these cells were resident.
>
>**Experiment** : Intra vascular staining and two-photon microscopy
>**Results** : Liver T cells were labelled, suggesting that they were blood exposed. Microscopy revealed that they were located at liver sinusoid with slow migration.
>
>**Experiment** : prime-and-trap vaccination stategy; peptide coupled to mAb anti-CLEC9A to target DC with poly(I:C) or CpG; then T cells are attracted to the liver by using recombinant adeno-associated virus that express the T cell antigen upon hepatocyte-specific alpha-1 antitrypsin promoter.
>**Results** : There was formation of liver TRM cells that protected upon challenge. CpG was more efficient than poly(I:C). Parabiosis confirmed residency. Trm had higher positive correlation between cell number and disease control than Tem.
>
>**Experiment** : Use of GFP-CX3CR1 and GFP-CXCR6 to track Tem and Trm, respectively.
>**Results** : Microscopy showed that Tem were rounder and travelled faster in the liver. Trm were amoeboid and migrated slowly in the sinusoids.
>
>**Experiment** : Use of anti-CXCR3 to specifically deplete TRM but not TEM (verified)
>**Results** : Treatement lead to the loss of protection; this was not lost in NKT-less deficient mice (because NKT also epxress CXCR3)
>
>**Experiment** : *ex-vivo* re-stimulation to study functionnality/cytotoxicity. Had to use nanobody s+16a here also.
>**Results** : TRM had better functionnality/cytotoxicity than TEM.
>
>**Experiment** : Use of a mathematical model to predict how much time and how many TRM are necessary for liver control. 2.5 Trm necessary for 99% coverage in 48 hours (mouse).
> %% end Notes %%
