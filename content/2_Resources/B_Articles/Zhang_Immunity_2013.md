## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article used TGFbRII KO P14 T cells in LCMV Armstrong/Cl13 infection mouse model. They found a small decrease in expansion of KO T cells and an increase of TGFbRII WT T cells in Cl13 vs Armstrong, but the mot interesting results was a decrease in TGFbRII KO cells in the intestine after Armstrong virus clearance. KO cells had decrease in CD69/CD103/CD49a/CD49d expression and reduced retention. Since there was no reduction in Cl13 TGFbRII KO cells, the authors tested and found that this was due to increase in CD49d/beta7 in KO cells in chronic infection, which lead to increase homing, but that KO T cells will decrease after virus clearing. [[TRM Differenciation]] [[TRM Phenotype]] [[TRM Caracteristics]] [[TRM Caracteristics]] 
> 
>**Experiment** : Co-transfer of WT/TGFbRII KO naive P14 T cells in mouse, then infection with LCMV Armstrong/Cl13.
>**Results** : There was more expression of WT TGFbRII in Cl13 infected mouse compared to Armstrong. There was small decrease in expansion of KO T cells, with comparable IFNg and IL2 production compared to WT. There was a decline in TGRbRII KO cells in the intestine after virus clearance (Armstrong) and to lesser degree in LP and no decrease in spleen. There as no difference in cell number in Cl13 infection. There was a reduction in CD69/CD103 and CD49d/CD49a expression in both acute and chronic infection, suggesting that although there was less expression of adherence molecule, this was not the reason for the decrease of TGFbRII KO T cells number in tissue observed in acute infection. The decrease in retention capacity was confirmed in both acute and chronic infection. This was interpreted as intestinal TRM being continuously recruited in the gut in WT or in chronic infection, and so the acute infection in the KO conditions has a decrease in TRM after virus clearance.
>
>**Experiment** : *in vitro* TGFb treatment
>**Results** : Decrease in alpha4beta7 expression.
>
>**Experiment** : Measure of homing capacities of KO/WT after infection
>**Results** : KO cells had much higher homing capacitiesin the chronic infection. This was lost when blocking CD49d when paired with integrin beta 7, which expression was greatly increase in TGFbRII KO cells.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Tissue-resident memory T (Trm) cells represent a population of memory CD8+ T cells that can act as first responders to local infection. The mechanisms regulating the formation and maintenance of intestinal Trm cells remain elusive. Here we showed that transforming growth factor-β (TGF-β) controlled both stages of gut Trm cell differentiation through different mechanisms. During the formation phase of Trm cells, TGF-β signaling inhibited the migration of effector CD8+ T cells from the spleen to the gut by dampening the expression of integrin α4β7. During the maintenance phase, TGF-β was required for the retention of intestinal Trm cells at least in part through the induction of integrins αEβ7 and α1, as well as CD69. Thus, the cytokine acts to control cytotoxic T cell differentiation in lymphoid and peripheral organs.
