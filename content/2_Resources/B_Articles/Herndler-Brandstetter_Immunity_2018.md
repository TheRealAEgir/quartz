## Notes

> [!notes]
>**Summary** : This article used *in vivo* fate mapping of KLRG1 to track the T cells that lose KLRG1 expression, and described that some KLRG1+ cells (mainly CD127+KLRG1+) still have some plasticity potential. This population showed a phenotype composed of both effector and memory characteristics and was able to survive for extended period of time. This population was able to differenciate into CD69+CD103- TRM, but not CD103+ TRM. There was some evidence that BACH2 was controlling this population, with high BACH2 expression resulting in exKLRG1 Tcm and Tem formation.
>[[TRM Precursors]] [[Other Memory and Effector Subsets]] [[Effector_Memory cell formation]]
>
> %% begin Notes %%
>**Model** : KLRG1cre system which enable tracking of KLRG1+ effector cells *in vivo*, by havind a GFP-cre fusion protein under the promoter of KLRG1, a reporter gene activated once the cre remove the floxed STOP codon in the middle of the reporter gene.
>**Results** : Cells that doesn't express KLRG1 doesn't have the reporter gene, validating the model.
>**Model** : Transfer of naive OT-I in KLRG1cre mice and infection with OVA-expressing listeria monocytogenes or VSV
>**Results** : OT-I cells started upregulating KLRG1 and the reporter gene at 4 dpi. The reporter was faithfull at 14 dpi, and there was development of exKLRG1 cells in the blood and spleen up to 20-35%. These population could formed long-lived cells at a similar rate to KLRG1- cells 120 dpi.
>
>**Other results** : exKLRG1 were detected in lung parenchyma after anti-CD8 injection while KLRG1+ were only detected in lung perivascular niche.
>
>**Other results** : exKLRG1 and KLRG1- expressed similar level of CD69 and were both dependent on IL15 in liver and lung. Oral infection lead to CD103 expression mainly in KLRG1- population. Sub-cutaneous injection of OVA lead to exKLRG1 recruitment in the skin.
>
>**Other results** : exKLRG1 expressed intermediate level of GZMB, KI67, BCL2 and TCF1, as well as ZEB2, PRDM1, BACH2. They also expressed CX3CR1 and re-expressed intermediate level of CD127 and CD62L after primary response.
>
>**Experiment** : ATACseq of the populations
>**Results** : exKLRG1 showed open chromatin for IL7R, IL2, TCF7, BACH2 and KLRG1, CX3CR1, GZMA (both effector and memory-related genes.)
>
>**Experiment** : Cell transfer of sorted KLRG1 populations
>**Results** : KLRG1+ reporter+/KLRG1+ reporter- and KLRG1- reporter- could give rise to exKLRG1 by day 14 PI. exKLRG1 from KLRG1+reporter+ cells displayed higher IL7R, CD62L and lower CX3CR1 that KLRG1+.
>
>**Experiment** : Transcription analysis of KLRG1 population
>**Results** : exKLRG1 resembled KLRG1- cells but not KLRG1+. exKLRG1 memory cells had an intermediate expression level of Tem cell-associated molecules (Gzma, Zeb2, Prdm1, S1pr5, Cx3cr1, and T-bet) and Tcm cell-associated molecules (Id3,Socs3, Bach2, Myc,Bcl-2, Eomes, CD62L, CXCR3, CD43, and CCR7)
>
>**Experiment** : Measure of the responsivness of exKLRG1 to cytokines
>**Results** : exKLRG1 had higher IFNg production in response to cytokine (antigen independent) that KLRG1-
>
>**Other Results** : exKLRG1 and KLRG1+ were the most efficient in anti-influenza immunity, with CD107a expression. In tumor settings, exKLRG1 and KLRG6- were the most efficient.
>
>**Experiment** : Tracking of BACH2 with BACH2flag and co-transfer of WT/KO or overexpression
>**Results** : BACH2 was downregulated in SLEC vs MPEC and DPEC. BACH2 lead to a decrease of all cell type 27 dpi, and of exKLRG1 memory in the spleen. Overexpression lead to impaired expension of effector and failed to express KLRG1. Similar observations in a model were BACH2 is removed by the expression of CRE under a KLRG1 promoter co transfered with the same cells with WT BACH2 in an infected naive mice.
>

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Protective immunity against pathogens depends on the efficient generation
of functionally diverse effector and memory T lymphocytes. However, whether
plasticity during effector-to-memory CD8+ T cell differentiation
affects memory lineage specification and functional versatility remains unclear.
Using genetic fate mapping analysis of highly cytotoxic KLRG1+
effector CD8+ T cells, we demonstrated that KLRG1+ cells
receiving intermediate amounts of activating and inflammatory signals,
downregulated KLRG1 during the contraction phase in a
Bach2-dependent manner, and differentiated into all memory T
cell linages, including CX3CR1int peripheral memory cells
and tissue-resident memory cells. ‘ExKLRG1’ memory cells retained
high cytotoxic and proliferative capacity distinct from other populations, which
contributed to effective anti-influenza and anti-tumor immunity. Our work
demonstrates that developmental plasticity of KLRG1+ effector
CD8+ T cells is important in promoting functionally versatile
memory cells and long-term protective immunity., Herndler-Brandstetter et al. demonstrate that KLRG1+
IL-7Rα+ effector CD8+ T cells downregulate
KLRG1 in a Bach2-depenent manner and differentiate into
long-lived circulating and tissue-resident ‘exKLRG1’ memory cells.
Developmental plasticity of KLRG1+ effector cells therefore drives
functional diversity within memory T cell lineages and promotes enhanced
anti-influenza and anti-tumor immunity.,
