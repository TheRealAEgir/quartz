## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : [[1.1 Réponse Mémoire]]
> 
>**Experiment** : Started on the observation that many TF expression was not different between TE and MP and that there must be other kind of regulation -> ATACseq and ChIPseq (H3K4ME1, H3K4ME3, H3K27AC, H3K27ME3) of sorted TE and MP from OT-I and LM-OVA infected mouse.
>**Results** : Tbet Histone modification reduced in Teff, apparition of TCF1 repression in Teff (H3K27ME3). Globally, higher H3K27ME3 in Teff. Also regulation of GZMA, IL7R, ID2, ID3. Applying clustering showed set of genes enriched in naive/TE/MP including KLRG1, TBX21, PRF1, IL7R, CXCR4. IL12 signature matched TE and TGFb/EGF pathways in naive/memory.
>
>**Other Results** : found a TBET motif near ZEB2. Naive repressed TBET/BATF/SREBP2/AP1, TE repressed TCF1/LEF1/E2A. TBET loss lead to ZEB2/GZMA/KLRB1 loss in Teff and BCL2/CRTAM/POU6F1 in MP.
>
>**Other Results** : PageRank rankings : STAT3 important in MP. Focused analysis on YY1 and NR3C1 which ranked high, even if their expression was not different
>**Experiment** : co-transfert of WT or shRNA treated T cells and LM-OVA infection
>**Results** : KD of YY1 lead to increase of MP associated molecules CD27 CXCR3 and TCF1, while KD of NR3C1 decreased them as well as CD127. NR3C1 KD also lead to decrease in MP.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Dynamic changes in the expression of transcription factors (TFs) can influence the specification of distinct CD8+ T cell fates, but the observation of equivalent expression of TFs among differentially fated precursor cells suggests additional underlying mechanisms. Here we profiled the genome-wide histone modifications, open chromatin and gene expression of naive, terminal-effector, memory-precursor and memory CD8+ T cell populations induced during the in vivo response to bacterial infection. Integration of these data suggested that the expression and binding of TFs contributed to the establishment of subset-specific enhancers during differentiation. We developed a new bioinformatics method using the PageRank algorithm to reveal key TFs that influence the generation of effector and memory populations. The TFs YY1 and Nr3c1, both constitutively expressed during CD8+ T cell differentiation, regulated the formation of terminal-effector cell fates and memory-precursor cell fates, respectively. Our data define the epigenetic landscape of differentiation intermediates and facilitate the identification of TFs with previously unappreciated roles in CD8+ T cell differentiation.
