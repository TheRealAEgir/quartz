## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article describe an accumulation of CXCR6+PD1+ CD8+, highly expanded, with a TRM signature, T cells in proximity to microglia the brain in alzeheimer disease. CXCR6 depletion lead to decrease of the infiltration and increased cell death, increased distance with microglial cells and higher number of Ab plates. CXCL16, TNFa and IL1b expression was higher on microglial cells. T cells showed inhibitory phenotype (CD73, LAG3, CD39, TIGIT) and inhibited microglial TNFa/Il1b secretion in a CD73 dependant manner. [[2. Hypothèses TIL]] [[2.4 Caractérisation TRM]]
> 
>**Experiment** : scRNAseq of CD45int/CD11b+ migroglia and CD45+CD11b- immune cells from brain of 5xFAD (alzheimer disease = AD) and control mouse
>**Results** : More CD8 T cells in brain AD, age-dependant, and reduction in meninges.
>**Experiment** : TCRa-/- or B2m KO mouse with no T cells
>**Results** : increased disease Ab plate deposition and mouse behavior.
>
>**Experiment** : CXCL16-CXCR6 interaction was infered to be important, so CXCL16 and CXCR6 expression analyses
>**Results** : Medium to high expression of CXCL16 by microglia, increase in AD mouse and in pathologic microglia. Validated in human from post-mortem samples. High CXCR6 expression in T cells compared to spleen.
>**Experiment** : CXCR6 KO mouse
>**Results** : Reduced CXCR6+ CD8+ T cells in the brains, normal proliferation but increased cell death. Seems important for survival. No effect of the type of Ab plates, but overall higher number of plate in the absence of CXCR6.
>
>**Experiment** : scRNA-TCRseq analyses
>**Results** : higher proportions of highly expanded T cells (n > 20) in AD mouse. PD1 correlated with TCR expansion, and there was an increase of PD1+ CD8+ T cells in the brain of AD mouse. These cells showed a TRM phenotype (CD69, CXCR6, CD103) and higher upregulation of CD69/CD103 in AD mouse. Intravascular injection of anti-CD45 Ab showed no staining of these cells. When CXCR6 is deleted, there is less clonally expanded T cells.
>
>**Experiment** : Analyses of the microglia/CD8 T cells interactions
>**Results** : Microglial cells expressed TNF and IL1B, suggesting pro-inflammatory. There is a co-localisation betwen PD1+ CD8+ T cells and migroglia, which is reduced in CXCR6 KO mouse. In CXCR6 or B2M KO mouse, there is a slight increase in TNF and proIL1B expression.
>**Other Results** : There is enichment of a regulatory CD8+ T cells gene signature in clonally expanded PD1+ CD8+ T cells. High expression of CD73, TIGIT, CD39 and LAG3.
>**Experiment** : *in vitro* co-culture
>**Results** : Reduction of TNFa and IL1b, increased expression of regulatory markers and CXCR6.
>**Experiment** : CD73 KO
>**Results** : Modestly decreased the previously observed results, suggesting CD73 have a role in CXCR6+ mediated inhibition of migroglia expression of TNFa and IL1B.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Neurodegenerative diseases, including Alzheimer’s disease (AD), are characterized by innate immune-mediated inflammation, but functional and mechanistic effects of adaptive immune system remain unclear. Here, we identify brain-resident CD8+ T cells that co-express CXCR6 and PD-1 and are in proximity to plaque-associated microglia in human and mouse AD brains. We also establish that CD8+ T cells restrict AD pathologies, including β-amyloid deposition and cognitive decline. Ligand–receptor interaction analysis identifies CXCL16–CXCR6 inter-cellular communication between microglia and CD8+ T cells. Further, Cxcr6 deficiency impairs accumulation, tissue residency programming, and clonal expansion of brain PD-1+CD8+ T cells. Ablation of Cxcr6 or CD8+ T cells ultimately increases pro-inflammatory cytokine production from microglia, with CXCR6 orchestrating brain CD8+ T cell–microglia co-localization. Collectively, our study reveals protective roles for brain CD8+ T cells and CXCR6 in mouse AD pathogenesis, and highlights that microenvironment-specific, inter-cellular communication orchestrates tissue homeostasis and protection from neuroinflammation.
