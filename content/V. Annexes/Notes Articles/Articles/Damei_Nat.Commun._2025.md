## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article studied T cell infiltration after anti-PD1 anti-CTLA4 treatment in a mouse model of tumor expressing E-cadherin and collagen IV. Anti-PD1 efficacy was dependant on the expansion of a CD8 T cell population that expressed CD103, and efficacy and *ex vivo* tumor killing was reduced by ant-CD103 neutralizing antibody. In a similar way, anti-CTLa4 efficacy was dependant on the expansion of a CD49a+ CD4+ TRM cluster and efficacy and *ex vivo* tumor killing was affected by anti-CD49a neutrlizing antibody. The CD4+ TRM cluster equivalent was found in human scRNAseq and IHC, where it's infiltration was associated with better ICB response, PSF and OS in human melanoma treated with anti-PD-1 anti-CTLA4. [[2.5 TRM Cancer]] [[2.4 Caractérisation TRM]] [[2.2 Fonctions TRM]] 
> 
>**Experiment** : MC38 expressing E-cadherin and type IV collagen mouse tumor model with anti-PD1 anti-CTLA4
>**Results** : Better protection and increase of a FOXP3neg CD4+ cell type with anti CTLA4. in scRNAseq, enriched cell had a TRM phenotype.
>
>**Other Results** : Anti-PD1 efficacy was abolished by anti-CD8 or anti-CD103 neutralizing ab, but not by anti-CD49a. co-culture efficacy was limited by anti-CD103 ab.
>
>**Other Results** : Anti-CTLA-4 increased a CD49a+ CD4+ TRM cluster and changed it's transcriptome. Anti-CD4 or anti-CD49a neutralizing ab compromise anti-CTLA4 efficacy. Anti-CTLA4 increased *ex vivo* coculture tumor killing, which was decreased by anti-CD49a, anti-collagen IV or anti-MHC-II neutralizing ab. CD49a CD4+ TRM were more functional than CD49a-.
>
>**Other Results** : Similar cluster found in human database and FC analyses.
>
>**Experiment** : IHC in ICB treated melanoma
>**Results** : increase in CD4 Teff and CD4 CD49a+ TRM in responders compared ot non responders, and CD49a TRMhigh patient had better PFS/OS
