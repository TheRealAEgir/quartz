## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article showed that TRM have a direct impact in antitumor immune response. They describe a vaccine strategy that generate TRM (validated using transcriptomic, phenotype, persistence after immunisation and without antigen, absence of staining in intravascular staining, and parabiosis). TRM showed IFNg secretion and CKI expression. TRM were directly involved in protection, as FTY720-treated mouse which doesn't have Teff nor Tcirc have protection; also, anti-TGFb abrogated TRM formation and resulted in impaired protection; finally, only immunised parabiont had TRM and showed protection. In human, CD8+CD103+ infiltration (with IF) was associated with higher OS. [[2.5 TRM Cancer]] [[2.3 Différenciation TRM]] [[2.7 TRM Thérapies]]
> 
>**Experiment** : Intranasal vaccination STxB-E7 (target DC)
>**Results** : Better CD8+ mediated protection compared to intramuscular in head and neck cancer orthotopic TC1 model.
>**Other Results** : These CD8+ T cells were TRM :  Transcriptomic analyses of E7-specific CD8 T cells in this setting showed that they expressed TRM genes. CD103 and CD49a were highly expressed and were both used as TRM markers. They persisted to 90 dp immunisation. They were not stained by intravascular staining. They secreted high IFNg in *ex vivo* E7 peptide stimulation and express PD1 and TIM3.
>
>**Experiment** : Tumor graft in immunised mouse with or without FTY720
>**Results** : Mouse were protected even when treated with FTY720 and when graft were done at 8 dpi (effector phase, Teff still in tumor) and at 30 dpi (only TRM in the tumor). There was a correlation between TRM number and protection. Immunised parabiont had higher protection than non-immunised.
>
>**Experiment** : Removing of TRM generation with anti-TGFb
>**Results** : TRM but not Teff were impacted by anti-TGFb, which in turn impacted protection.
>**Experiment** : Parabiosis with an immunised and non-immunised mice, then tumor graft
>**Results** : Same level of E7-specific CD8 T cells in spleen, but only E7-specific TRM in immunised mouse, suggesting residency.
>
>**Experiment** : IF in lung cancer patients
>**Results** : CD103+ CD8+ T cells were localised in epithelial regions. Smokers had higher infiltrate. CD8+/CD103+ intratumoral T cell infiltration was associated with better survival.
>**Other Results** : Correlation between CD8A and ITGAE epxression and mutation burden in TCGA, but not with survival.
>
>
L'une des premiers travaux décris une stratégie de vaccination intra-nasale capable de générer des TRM [[Nizard_NatCommun_2017]]. Ces cellules présentent un phénotype et un transcriptome de résidence,. Dans ce modèle, la présence de TRM corrèle avec le niveau de protection, et le rôle direct des TRM a été validé de trois façons différentes. D'abord, les souris sont protégées en absence de Teff (phase mémoire) et de Tcirc (traitements avec du FTY720). Ensuite, le traitement avec un anti-TGFb bloque la génération de TRM, et impacte la protection des souris. Finalement, l'utilisation de la parabiose montre que seule le parabiont immunisé au préalable, mais pas l'hôte naïf, est protégé contre une greffe de tumeur [[Nizard_NatCommun_2017]].

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Tissue-resident memory T cells (Trm) represent a new subset of long-lived memory T cells that remain in tissue and do not recirculate. Although they are considered as early immune effectors in infectious diseases, their role in cancer immunosurveillance remains unknown. In a preclinical model of head and neck cancer, we show that intranasal vaccination with a mucosal vector, the B subunit of Shiga toxin, induces local Trm and inhibits tumour growth. As Trm do not recirculate, we demonstrate their crucial role in the efficacy of cancer vaccine with parabiosis experiments. Blockade of TFGβ decreases the induction of Trm after mucosal vaccine immunization, resulting in the lower efficacy of cancer vaccine. In order to extrapolate this role of Trm in humans, we show that the number of Trm correlates with a better overall survival in lung cancer in multivariate analysis. The induction of Trm may represent a new surrogate biomarker for the efficacy of cancer vaccine. This study also argues for the development of vaccine strategies designed to elicit them.
