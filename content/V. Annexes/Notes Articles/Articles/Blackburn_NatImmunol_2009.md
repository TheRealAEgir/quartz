## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article described that exhaustion inhibition is caused by non-redundant co-expression of ICB [[1.3 Dysfonction]]
> 
>**Experiment** : Transcriptomics of naive, Teff, Tmem and Tex
>**Results** : PD1 had similar pattern of expression and correlated with 2B4, CD160, CTLA4 and LAG3. Verified in FC.
>
>**Experiment** : Different severity of infection by combining Cl13, T1B, Arm +- CD4 depletion
>**Results** : ICP expression. Number of different ICB expressed correlated with severity and inversely correlated with secretion of IFNg and TNFa. PD1 > LAG3 > 2B4 > CD160.
>
>**Experiment** : anti-PD1, anti-LAG3, anti CD160 or anti-CD48 (2B4 ligand) treatement, efficacy measured by intro killing, in vitro apoptosis and cytokine production
>**Results** : Depend on experiment and treatement. Overall, increased efficacy.
>
>**Experiment** : in vivo anti-PD1 + anti-LAG3
>**Results** : anti-LAG3 alone did not have effect, but increased the efficacy of the anti-PD-L1 : increase of number of antigen-specific T cells, functionnality (IFN and CD107 expression) and viral load.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > T cell exhaustion often occurs during chronic infection and prevents optimal viral control. The molecular pathways involved in T cell exhaustion remain poorly understood. Here we show that exhausted CD8+ T cells are subject to complex layers of negative regulation resulting from the coexpression of multiple inhibitory receptors. Exhausted CD8+ T cells expressed up to seven inhibitory receptors. Coexpression of multiple distinct inhibitory receptors was associated with greater T cell exhaustion and more severe infection. Regulation of T cell exhaustion by various inhibitory pathways was nonredundant, as blockade of the T cell inhibitory receptors PD-1 and LAG-3 simultaneously and synergistically improved T cell responses and diminished viral load in vivo. Thus, CD8+ T cell responses during chronic viral infections are regulated by complex patterns of coexpressed inhibitory receptors.
