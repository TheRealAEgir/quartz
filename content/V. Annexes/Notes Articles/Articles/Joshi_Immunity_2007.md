## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : First article to describe SLEC/MPEC classification based on KLRG1/CD127 expression [[1.1 Réponse Mémoire]]
> 
>**Experiment** : P14 transfer in naïve mouse then LCMV infection and transcriptomics of sorted IL7hi vs low
>**Results** : DEG = KLRG1, Ly49c, KLRE1, 2B4, Ly49h. KLRG1hiCD127lo contracted after resolution, whereas KLRG1loCD127hi did not. When sorted population were co-transfered, KLRG1loCD127hi better survived.
>
>**Experiment** : IL15-/- mouse
>**Results** : SLEC rapidly disappeared, suggesting a role of IL15 in persistance of SLEC.
>
>**Experiment** : kinetics
>**Results** : KLRG1 appeared between 4-5 dpi, while CD127 decreased at 4-5 dpi before increasing a 6-8 dpi.
>
>**Other Results** : More CD27 and CD122 on CD127hi. Comparable IFNg secretion and cytotoxicity, and proliferation, but only CD127hi secreted IL2. Interfering with KLRG1 with shRNA did not change anything.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of gp33-DC with different doses of LM which induces inflammation
>**Results** : There was a direct correlation between SLEC formation and inflammation through gp33-DC-LM.
>**Other Results** : Pre-treatment of P14 T cells with IL12 w/o IFNG increased SLEC number and Tbet expression.
>
>**Experiment** : Tbet-/- mouse or co-transfer of WT/Tbet KO in mouse.
>**Results** : There was a lot less Teff, which were not impacted functionnaly but increased IL2 secretion. They had a phenotype that looked closer to MPEC (CD27h, Ly49c/KLRE1/2B4-). Tbet KO failed to induce KLRG1hiCD127low upon IL12 treatment. This was restored by tbet encoding retrovirus injection.
>
>**Other Results** : Tbet was required for CD122 expression.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > As acute infections resolve, effector CD8 T cells differentiate into IL-7Rlo short-lived effector cells (SLECs) and IL-7Rhi memory precursor effector cells (MPECs) capable of generating long-lived memory CD8 T cells. Using another SLEC marker, KLRG1, we found that KLRG1hi effector cells began appearing early during infection and were committed to downregulating IL-7R. Unlike IL-7Rhi MPECs, KLRG1hi IL-7Rlo SLECs relied on IL-15, but IL-15 could not sustain their long-term maintenance or homeostatic turnover. The SLEC/MPEC fate decision was regulated by the amount of inflammatory cytokines (i.e. IL-12) present during T cell priming. According to the amount of inflammation, a gradient of T-bet was created in which high T-bet levels induced SLECs and low levels promoted MPECs. These results elucidate a novel mechanism by which the innate immune system sets the relative levels of a lineage-determining transcription factor in activated CD8 T cells and correspondingly, regulates their memory cell potential.
