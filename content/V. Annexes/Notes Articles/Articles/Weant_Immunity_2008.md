## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : BIM KO mouse lead to autoimmune disease by presence of self-reactive peripheral cells Bouillet et al. 1999). Fas play a role in eliminating self-reactive T cells. BIM is intrinsic and FAS is extrinsic apoptosis pathways.
> This article crossed BIM KO mouse with FAS KO mouse and observed an accumulation of T cells. This was not due to increase in proliferation but lower apoptosis and AICD. The mouse developped auto-immune syndrome with increase blood antibody titers and renal infiltration. Finally, while this could be due to increase in thymocyte selection, it also was true with LCMV Arm infection and antigen-specific T cells. [[1.1 Réponse Mémoire]]
> 
>**Experiment** : BIM KO FAS KO DKO mouse
>**Results** : lymphadenopathy at 14-22 weeks. More splenocytes and liver/LN total lymphocytes (10 in spleen/liver and 100 in LN).
>**Other Results** : DP thymocytes were not altered. There was more CD44hi T cells. 
>
>**Other Results** : There is no differences in proliferation assessed by BrdU uptake. The cells from DKO mice did not show apoptosis assessed by 7AAD. WT cells died quicker *ex vivo*, and DKO cells were resistant to AICD.
>
>**Other Results** : DKO mouse had higher blood antibody titer and anti-dsDNA ab and renal infiltrate, suggesting auto-immune syndrome.
>
>**Experiment** : LCMV Arm infection in DKO mouse
>**Results** : Increase of antigen-specific T cells in LN compare to single KO or WT. FAS single KO had also more effect than BIM single KO. This was not due to increase in proliferation.
> %% end Notes %%
