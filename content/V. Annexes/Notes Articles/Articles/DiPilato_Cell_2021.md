## Notes

> [!notes]
> %% begin Notes %%**Summary** : This article describe a role for CXCR6 in the survival of effector like CTL in tumors. This is mediated with anti-apoptotic signal mediated by DC3 presented IL15. [[2. Hypothèses TIL]] [[2.4 Caractérisation TRM]]
> 
>**Experiment** : scRNAseq of murine tumor model
>**Results** : CD8 memory and effector like. Eff expressed CXCR6. When PD-1 is used as a antigen specific marker, it is expressed on the progeny of CD62L+ T cells, and PD-1+ T cells express CXCR6, suggesting that CXCR6 expression is sustained by TCR activation.
>
>**Experiment** : tumor implantation into WT or CXCR6 KO mouse
>**Results** : acceleration of tumor growth. Most CXCR6 expression was on T cells. When doing BM mixing, there is a loss of CXCR6 KO in the PD-1+ TILs, but not in TCF1+. There was a slight delay in proliferation and CD69/CD25 acquisition in CXCR6 cells, suggesting a minor role in priming. However, the contraction after 7 days was much higher in the CXCR6 KO population, suggesting a role in maintenance. Also, less Ki67 expression (mostly in TCF1-) and less BCL2.
>
>**Experiment** : To study CXCR6+ T cell origin, use of in vitro differentiated TCF1+/TCF1- like (that uses IL2/IL12) and transfer into mouse, then tumor inoculation.
>**Results** : Only TCF1neg expressed CXCR6 and remained TCF1neg.
>
>**Experiment** : Transfer of WT or CXCR6 KO TCF1neg like cells into mouse bearing tumors
>**Results** : CXCR6 deficient T cells are devoid of anti-tumor activity. 2 days after the beginning, there was more CXCR6 KO T cells in tumors, while after 3 days this was the opposite until days 4-5. CXCR6 KO showed apoptosis. Apoptosis was not the result of AICD, as the use of antigen-deficient tumor cell line did not show any different between the groups. If apopostis is prevented by retroviral transduction of BCL2, there is less apoptosis and tumor control is restored.
>
>**Experiment** : CXCL16 expression
>**Results** : Highest in CCR7+ DC3 in scRNAseq
>
>**Experiment** : Co-transfer of WT or CXCR6 KO T cells intro DC3-reporter mouse with tumors
>**Results** : More WT in proximity of not than CXCR6 KO. When normalizing, there is less CXCR6 KO in contact to DC3.
>**Experiment** : Mouse model were DC3 can be depleted by DT treatement. Same experiment
>**Results** : Increase in WT apoptosis and the ratio of CXCR6 KO/WT after DC3 depletion.
>
>**Experiment** : *in vitro* IL15 treatment
>**Results** : No increase in CXCR6, but increase if there is a IL12 conversion to TCF1neg like before.
>**Experiment** : Co-transfer of WT or CXCR6 KO TCF1neg like T cells in WT or IL15 KO mouse
>**Results** : Increase in WT apoptosis in IL15 KO mouse, and BIM expression correlated with this result. It was verified that IL15 is expressed by DC3 and that DC3 depletion have similar results.
>
>**Other results** : CXCR6 expression associated with better survival in TCGA melanoma, HNSCC, BC, lung.

> > [!Abstract]-
> >
> > Cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL) responses against tumors are maintained by stem-like memory cells that self-renew but also give rise to effector-like cells. The latter gradually lose their anti-tumor activity and acquire an epigenetically fixed, hypofunctional state, leading to tumor tolerance. Here, we show that the conversion of stem-like into effector-like CTLs involves a major chemotactic reprogramming that includes the upregulation of chemokine receptor CXCR6. This receptor positions effector-like CTLs in a discrete perivascular niche of the tumor stroma that is densely occupied by CCR7+ dendritic cells (DCs) expressing the CXCR6 ligand CXCL16. CCR7+ DCs also express and trans-present the survival cytokine interleukin-15 (IL-15). CXCR6 expression and IL-15 trans-presentation are critical for the survival and local expansion of effector-like CTLs in the tumor microenvironment to maximize their anti-tumor activity before progressing to irreversible dysfunction. These observations reveal a cellular and molecular checkpoint that determines the magnitude and outcome of anti-tumor immune responses.
