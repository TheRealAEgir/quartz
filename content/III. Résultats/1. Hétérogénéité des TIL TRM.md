## I. Patient with poor prognosis have higher [[CXCR6]] expression on CD103+CD69+ [[TRM]] isolated from CRC liver metastases

We have previously demonstrated that CD103+CD69+ [[TRM]] cells proportion correlates with improved overall survival (OS) in CRC patients with liver metastasis (CRCLM)[[Abdeljaoued_Oncoimmunology_2025]]. Since [[TRM]] are a heterogeneous population [[Cheuk_Immunity_2017]] [[Christo_NatImmunol_2021]] [[Obers_Immunity_2024]] [[Reina-Campos_Nature_2025]], , we tested if [[TRM]] subtypes could be associated with different clinical outcome. As we already reported, [[PD-1]] expression on [[TRM]] cells was associated with decreased recurrence rate [[Abdeljaoued_Oncoimmunology_2025]]. However, we also found high heterogeneity in the [[CXCR6]] expression on [[TRM]] cells (Figure 1 [[Figure 1 A.png|A]] [[Figure 1 B.png|B]] [[Figure 1 C.png|C]] [[Figure 1 D.png|D]]). Surprisingly, high expression of [[CXCR6]] on CD103+CD69+ [[TRM]] cells was significantly associated with a shorter recurrence free survival (RFS) (p = 0.037) ([[Figure 1 E.png|Figure 1 E]]). Combination of both [[CXCR6]] and [[PD-1]] expression led us to the description of four [[TRM]] new subtypes, each being distributed almost equitably ([[Figure 1 F.png|Figure 1 F]]). Interestingly, the proportion of CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] were associated with decreased risk of relapse ([[Figure 1 G.png|Figure 1 G]]). This was not explained solely by [[PD-1]] expression as the proportion of CXCR6+PD-1+ [[TRM]] did not reached significance. This analysis also pointed to the CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] population which proportion was associated with increased risk of relapse ([[Figure 1 F.png|Figure 1 F]]). These results revealed a substantial phenotypic heterogeneity of CD103+CD69+ [[TRM]] cells according to [[CXCR6]] and [[PD-1]] expression associated with clinical value of CRC patients with liver metastases.
