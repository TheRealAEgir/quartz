## VI. Intra-tumoral CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] are enriched in ICB-responsive patients and display tumor reactivity

Intra-tumoral [[TRM]] are heterogenous in terms of tumor antigen recognition capacity, leading to the existence of tumor antigen-reactive and bystanders [[TRM]] cells3. Accordingly, we hypothesized that the [[TRM]] subtypes might differ in terms of tumor reactivity. Tumor reactivity cannot be tested using _in vitro_ cytokine stimulation where residency program is acquired in an antigen-independent manner. To be able to test tumor antigen-reactivity, we used a published pan-cancer atlas of single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNAseq) consisting of 30 cancer types, 999 treatment-naïve patients and XXX [[TRM]] cells. We took advantage of the bulk RNAseq experiment to derive population-specific gene signature that was then used for the annotation (see Extended Data Figure [[Figure Supplémentaire 4|4]] and [[Matériel et méthodes|Methods]]) to obtain putative [[TRM]] subsets cell annotations (Figure 5 [[Figure 6 A.png|A]]).

As expected, CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] cells had higher score of the four article-derived gene signatures of T cell tumor reactivity compared to CXCR6+PD-1- [[TRM]] cells (Figure 5 [[Figure 6 B.png|B]]). These results were reinforced by the localization of both subsets, where CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] cells were enriched in tumor sample compared to adjacent healthy tissue. Conversely, CXCR6+PD-1- [[TRM]] cells were enriched in adjacent healthy tissue (Figure 5 [[Figure 6 C.png|C]]).  Noteworthy, CXCR6+PD-1- [[TRM]] cells were more abundant in cold and aggressive tumors such as pancreatic cancer, brain tumors and neuroendocrine tumors (Extended Data Figure [[Figure Supplémentaire 5]]).  To further confirm these observations, we employed spatial transcriptomics coupled with protein expression Lunaphore technology. Importantly, this technology allowed us to define [[TRM]] subset with protein expression. Lunaphore analyses showed that CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] cells were mainly localized in the tumor center, whereas CXCR6+PD-1- [[TRM]] cells were essentially at the perilesional normal tissue on a cohort of liver metastases (Figure 5 [[Figure 6 D.png|D]]). Taken together, these results strongly suggest that CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] cells represent a tumor reactive [[TRM]] population in the TME.

Another major characteristic that define immune cell function in a microenvironment is cell-cell communication. To test this, we inferred cell-cell communication networks using the CellChat package. Despite relative similarity in cell communication between the two subtypes, we observed CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] cells had stronger incoming and outgoing interaction scores in the TME globally (Extended Data Figure [[Figure Supplémentaire 6|6]]). Interestingly, it also showed significantly stronger incoming and outgoing interaction scores among B cells, TH1, TFH, DCs and NK cells hinting that CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] cells foster a favorable immunogenic microenvironment (Extended Data Figure 6 [[Figure Supplémentaire 6 B|B]]-[[Figure Supplémentaire C|C]]).

We then thought to evaluate the importance of CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] cells infiltration in response to [[ICB]] treatment. We analyzed an additional 5 datasets of [[ICB]] treated patients (anti-PD-1, anti-CTLA-4 or anti-LAG3) (Figure 5 [[Figure 6 E.png|E]]). Consistent with our previous findings, CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] cells infiltration was significantly enriched in responders compared to non-responders, while non-responders presented higher infiltration with CXCR6+PD-1- [[TRM]] cells compared to responders (Figure 5 [[Figure 6 F.png|F]]). Both [[TRM]] populations infiltrated the tumors before and after treatment with [[ICB]]. However, CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] cells infiltration was highly increased during or after treatment, reminiscent of [[TPEX]] cells (Figure 5 [[Figure 6 G.png|G]]). Tumor reactivity signature scores showed similar trends to the atlas dataset (Figure 5 [[Figure 6 H.png|H]]).

Finally, we wanted to investigate the relevance of CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] in the clonal replacement following [[ICB]] responses. To do this, we defined _de novo_ infiltration as expanded clonotypes (n > 5) that is not observed in pre-treatment samples19. This way, we observed a significant higher proportion of clonal replacement CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] cells than of CXCR6+PD-1- [[TRM]] cells among patient in the GSE288199 dataset, while a similar yet not significant trend was observed in the GSE236581 dataset (Figure 5 [[Figure 6 I.png|I]]). Together, these observations suggest that CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] cells are involved in clonal replacement following [[ICB]] response.
