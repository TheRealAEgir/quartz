## V. CXCR6-PD-1+ TRM-like cells present a stem-like phenotype with higher proliferation capacity

To better understand the identity of the two TRM-like subsets, populations were sorted and RNA sequencing (RNAseq) was performed. Analysis of the differentially expressed genes (DEG) highlighted strong heterogeneity, with 578 genes upregulated in CXCR6+PD-1- and 172 upregulated genes in CXCR6+PD-1- TRM-like subsets (adjusted p-value < 0.05 and absolute log2 fold change > 0.5). Among DEG for the CXCR6-PD-1+ population were genes associated with stem-like and memory properties such as _TCF7_ (encoding [[TCF1]]), _ID3_, _IL7R_, _BACH2_, _CD27_, _CD28_ and _TNFRSF9_ (encoding [[4-1BB]]) (figure 4 [[Figure 5 A.png|A]]). On sharp contrast and despite not being activated by a chronic antigen, the CXCR6+PD1- population presented a transcriptional program consistent with T cell exhaustion, with the expression of several inhibitory receptor genes (such as _LAG3_, _TIGIT_, _KLRB1_ (CD160) or _CD244_ (encoding [[2B4]])). It also showed higher expression of several other key exhaustion-associated genes such as _ENTPD1_ (encoding [[CD39]]), _SLAMF7_, _ID2_ and _TOX2_ (Figure 4 [[Figure 5 A.png|A]]).

To confirm these data at the protein level, we then assessed the expression of biomarkers of T cell exhaustion and stemness in both subsets. CXCR6+PD1- TRM-like exhibited elevated expression of [[CD39]] and [[TIGIT]] (Figure 4 [[Figure 5 B.png|B]], Extended data Figure 4 [[Figure Supplémentaire 3 A.png|A]]). Conversely, CXCR6-PD1+ TRM-like cells, exhibited high expression of stemness biomarkers ([[TCF1]], BACH2 and [[CD127]]) (Figure 4 [[Figure 5 B.png|B]], Extended data Figure 4 [[Figure Supplémentaire 3 B.png|B]]). Since the expression of these proteins are affected by [[TCR]] stimulation, we assessed their expression in a kinetic _in vitro_ stimulation model. Consistently, CXCR6+PD1- TRM-like cells progressively acquired an exhaustion signature, upregulating [[CD39]], [[LAG3]] and SLAMF7 while downregulating [[TCF1]], BACH2 and [[CD127]] expression (Figure 4 [[Figure 5 C.png|C]], Extended data Figure 4 [[Figure Supplémentaire 3 C.png|C]]). Meanwhile, CXCR6-PD1+ TRM-like cells conserved higher expression of stemness transcription factors such as BACH2 and [[TCF1]] (Figure 4 [[Figure 5 C.png|C]]). Using the combination of [[TCF1]] and [[TOX]] expression as a proxy to evaluate exhaustion level, we observed an enrichment of TCF1+TOX- cells in the CXCR6-PD-1+ population while CXCR6+PD-1- had higher proportion of TCF1-TOX+ (Figure 4 [[Figure 5 D.png|D]]).

Loss of proliferation is a key feature in exhaustion [[Baessler_AnnuRevImmunol_2024]]. To verify if the stem-like CXCR6-PD-1+ population was protected against exhaustion, we assessed proliferation using [[Ki67]] expression. We observed higher [[Ki67]] expression in CXCR6-PD1+ TRM-like cells following activation, supporting a higher stem-like properties (Figure 4 [[Figure 5 E.png|E]] and Extended data Figure 4 [[Figure Supplémentaire 3 D.png|D]]-[[Figure Supplémentaire 3 E.png|E]]). We further validated these findings in a tumor spheroid model using MSS and MSI cancer cell lines and a bi-specific antibody T cell activation (Figure 4 [[Figure 5 F.png|F]]), demonstrating that CXCR6-PD1+ TRM-like cells maintained a sustained proliferation capacity and self-renewal even in a suppressive environment (Figure 4 [[Figure 5 G.png|G]]).

Overall, our data demonstrate that the CXCR6-PD1+ subset represents a population of stem-like TRM-like cells with high proliferative potential reminiscent of progenitor exhausted T cells ([[TPEX]]).  On the opposite, CXCR6+PD1- TRM-like cells displayed transcriptional and phenotypic features of exhaustion, with impaired self-renewal capacity.
