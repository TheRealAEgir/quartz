## VII. Exhausted T cells differentiate into CXCR6+PD-1- cells

Our initial results suggested that the “differentiation capacity” of [[TCIRC]] into TRM-like was linked to the clinical outcome of patients. Accordingly, we next thought to better understand how CXCR6-PD-1+ [[TRM]] differentiate and what factors drive this process. Previous results indicated that CXCR6+PD-1- TRM-like cells show signs of early exhaustion, including expression of multiple inhibitory checkpoints and restricted stem-like capacities, hinting that exhaustion might be related to this population. To test if exhaustion is involved in CXCR6+PD-1- cell differentiation, we generated TRM-like using exhausted T cells (see [[Matériel et méthodes#Génération de T<sub>RM</sub>-like *in vitro*, épuisement *in vitro* des lymphocytes et traitements avec les cytokines|Methods]]) or non-exhausted control conditions (Figure 6 [[Figure 7 A.png|A]]). Strikingly, we observed a near-complete loss of the generation of CXCR6-PD-1+ and a marked increase in the generation of CXCR6+PD-1- TRM-like cells in the exhausted compared to the non-exhausted control conditions (Figure 6 [[Figure 7 B.png|B]]-[[Figure 7 C.png|C]]).

These results suggested that the two subtypes have a distinct cell differentiation process. Notably, we did observe distinct cell trajectories using pseudotime analyses from the previous atlas of scRNAseq (Extended Data Figure [[Figure Supplémentaire 7|7]]). Seeking to better understand the underlying differentiation mechanism, we conducted a scRNAseq and TCRseq on cells under the previous conditions (Figure 6 [[Figure 7 A.png|A]] and [[Figure 7 D.png|D]]). As expected, mRNA expression of [[CXCR6]] was enriched in the exhausted conditions, but the expression of PDCD1 (encoding [[PD-1]]) was too low in all conditions (Extended Data Figure [[Figure Supplémentaire 8]]). Using the same annotation method as before (see Methods), we observed that again, CXCR6+PD-1- TRM-like cells were enriched when [[TRM]] generation was performed on exhausted T cells, while CXCR6-PD-1+ were only found under non-exhausted conditions (Figure 6 [[Figure 7 D.png|D]]).
