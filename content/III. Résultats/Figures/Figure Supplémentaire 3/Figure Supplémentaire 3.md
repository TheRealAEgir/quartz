###### Figure S 3 : CXCR6-PD1+ TRM-like retain phenotypical and transcriptional stem-like features
![](III.%20Résultats/Figures/Figure%20Supplémentaire%203/Figure%20Supplémentaire%203.png)
*TRM-like were differenciated from health donor blood [[TCIRC]]. (A-C) Expression of exhaustion-associated protein (A) or stemness-associated protein (B) was assessed using flow cytometry at steady-state or after a single anti-CD3 anti-CD28 Dynabeads™ activation in a kinetic manner (C). (D-F) Expression of the proliferation biormaker [[Ki67]] in TRM-like at steady state (D) or after a single anti-CD3 anti-CD28 Dynabeads™ activation at 48 hours (E) or in a kinetic manner (F). Results are represented as median ± SEM, with displayed p value of a Wilcoxon test for the given CXCR6+PD1- vs CXCR6-PD1+ pair. See also [[III. Résultats/Figures/Figure 4/Figure 4|Figure 4]]*
