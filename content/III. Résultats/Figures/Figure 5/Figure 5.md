###### Figure 5 : CXCR6-PD1+ TRM-like retain phenotypical and transcriptional stem-like features
![](III.%20Résultats/Figures/Figure%205/Figure%205.png)
*TRM-like were differenciated from cancer patients blood [[TCIRC]]. (A-B) Expression of exhaustion-associated protein (in purple) or stemness-associated protein (in green) was assessed using flow cytometry at steady-state (A) or after a single anti-CD3 anti-CD28 Dynabeads™ activation in a kinetic manner (B). (C) Proportion of TCF1+TOX- (TPEX-like) or TCF1-TOX+ (TEX-TERM-like) TRM-like was assessed using flow cytometry. (E) Heatmap representation of all significantly differentially expressed genes in bulk RNA sequencing of sorted CXCR6-PD1+ and CXCR6+PD1- TRM-like. Only gene of interest are labeled in the right side. (F-G) Expression of the proliferation biormaker [[Ki67]] in TRM-like at steady state (F) or after a single anti-CD3 anti-CD28 Dynabeads™ activation at 48 hours (F) or in a kinetic manner (G). Expression of [[Ki67]] in TRM-like in a co-culture model with Colo205 (left) of HCT116 (right) cell lines after 16 hours activation with a anti-CD3/anti-EPCAM bispecific T cell engager solitomab. Results are represented as median ± SEM, with displayed p value of a Wilcoxon test for the given CXCR6+PD1- vs CXCR6-PD1+ pair. See also [[Figure Supplémentaire 3|Supplementary Figure S3]]*
