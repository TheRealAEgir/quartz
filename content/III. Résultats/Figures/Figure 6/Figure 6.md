###### Figure 6 : In vivo CXCR6-PD1+-like [[TRM]] are enriched in immune-checkpoint inhibitor responsive patient in several cancer types
![](III.%20Résultats/Figures/Figure%206/Figure%206.png)
*Publicly accessible scRNAseq datasets were downloaded from GSEXXX (Kang et al.), GSE236581 (Chen et al.), GSE288199 (Housaiyin et al.), GSE176021 (Caushi et al.), GSE179994 (Liu et al.) and GSE123814 (Yost et al.) and re-analysed (see methods). [[TRM]] subtypes were annotated using gene signatures derived from bulk RNAseq of sorted TRM-like populations (see figure S 4 and methods). (A) UMAP representation of [[TRM]] colored by [[TRM]] subtypes from Kang et al. (B) Tumor reactivity gene signatures were retrieved from Caushi et al, Hanada et al, Lowery et al and Oliveira et al, and ssGSEA score was calculated. Violin plot representation of relative ssGSEA scores of CXCR6+PD-1- and CXCR6-PD-1- [[TRM]] in shown in B. (C) Proportion of CXCR6+PD-1- and CXCR6-PD-1- [[TRM]] according to tissue. (D) Relative localisation of  CXCR6+PD-1- and CXCR6-PD-1- [[TRM]] in lunaphore technology. (E) Schematic representation of important information regarding the used data. (F) UMAP representation of [[TRM]] colored by [[TRM]] subtypes and splited by patient’s response to [[ICB]] from ICB-treated databases. All distributions are statistically different between the responders (R) and non-responder (NR) according to a χ2 test with a Bonferroni correction for multiple testing. (H) Proportion of CXCR6+PD-1- and CXCR6-PD-1- [[TRM]] according to treatment timepoint. (I) Violin plot representation of relative ssGSEA scores of tumor reactivity gene signatures on CXCR6+PD-1- and CXCR6-PD-1- [[TRM]]. (J) Boxplot representation of clonal replacement cells among indicated [[TRM]] subtypes per patient. Clonal replacement was defined as follow : for each patient, a cell that has a [[TCR]] CDR3 nucleotide sequence being found more than 5 times in the tumor compartment in on/post [[ICB]] treatment sample and not found in pre-ICB treatment sample.*
