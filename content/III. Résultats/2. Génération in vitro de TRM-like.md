## I. _In vitro_ generated TRM-like cells recapitulate phenotypic and transcriptomic features of tumor infiltrating [[TRM]] cells

Response to [[ICB]] rely on a _de novo_ T cell repertoire distinct from pre-treatment T cells, which origin may be blood circulation [[Yost_NatMed_2019]]. We hypothesised that newly recruited circulating T cells ([[TCIRC]]) could differentiate into [[TRM]] with known anti-tumor efficiency, and so that the circulating T cell “differentiation capacity” might predict outcome. To mimic the differentiation of [[TCIRC]] entering the tumor into [[TRM]], peripheral blood-derived CD8+ T cells were differentiated _in vitro_ using TRM-inducing conditions, yielding a population with a TRM-like phenotype characterised by up regulation of [[CD69]], [[CD103]] and [[CXCR6]] expression. The nature and resemblance of obtained TRM-like cells was characterised using flow cytometry and bulk RNAseq, where we could observe expression of residency protein and validate similarity to tumor infiltrating [[TRM]] cells existing gene signature (Figure 2 [[Figure 2 A.png|A]]-[[Figure 2 B.png|B]]).
