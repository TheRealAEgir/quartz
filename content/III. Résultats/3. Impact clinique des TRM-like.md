## III. Differentiation of [[TCIRC]] into CXCR6-PD-1+ TRM-like cells is associated with response to immune checkpoint blockade

To test if differentiation capacity was associated with clinical outcome, we performed _in vitro_ generation of TRM-like from pre-treatment blood samples of ICB-treated MSIHigh metastatic CRC patient (Figure 2 [[Figure 3 A.png|A]]). Contrary to our initial thoughts , we observed that generation of TRM-like (defined as CD8+CD69+CD103+/- T cells) was not associated with neither response to [[ICB]] nor the patient prognosis (Figure 2 [[Figure 3 B.png|B]]). However, given that [[CXCR6]] and [[PD-1]] expression on tumor [[TRM]] was associated with recurrence state of CRCLM patients, we next examined the association of these biomarkers to treatment response. Interestingly, we observed higher expression of [[CXCR6]] on CD103+CD69+ TRM-like cells in samples with [[ICB]] resistance on two independent cohorts: MSIHigh metastatic CRC (NIPICOL) and MSIHigh gastrointestinal cancers (NEONIPIGA) treated with anti-PD-1 and anti-CTLA4 combination (Figure 2 [[Figure 3 C.png|C]]). In contrast to [[CXCR6]], [[PD-1]] expression on _in vitro_ obtained [[TRM]] cells was not associated with neither [[ICB]] response nor prognosis of patients (Figure 2?).

We next directed our focus on the additive value of [[CXCR6]] and [[PD-1]] expression on CD103+CD69+ TRM-like cells. In line with our previous findings, the proportion of CXCR6-PD-1+ TRM-like cells was associated with [[ICB]] response in MSIhigh metastatic CRC, whereas proportion of CXCR6+PD-1- TRM-like cells was associated with [[ICB]] resistance (Figure 2 [[Figure 3 D.png|D]]). Additionally, patient with higher proportion of obtained CXCR6-PD-1+ TRM-like cells had longer OS and progression free survival (PFS), contrarily to CXCR6+PD-1- TRM-like cells (Figure 2 [[Figure 3 F.png|F]]). To establish whether this was specific to [[ICB]] rather than a general prognostic feature, we performed the same experiment with samples from metastatic MSS CRC patients who had not received [[ICB]]. Notably, the propensity of [[TCIRC]] to differentiate into CXCR6-PD-1+ TRM-like cells was not associated with the prognosis of ICB-naïve patients, supporting a specific relationship between differentiation potential and response to [[ICB]] (Figure 2 [[Figure 3 G.png|G]]). Taken together, our findings indicate that the capacity of pre-treatment circulating CD8+ cells to differentiate into CXCR6-PD-1+ TRM-like cells is associated with response to [[ICB]].
