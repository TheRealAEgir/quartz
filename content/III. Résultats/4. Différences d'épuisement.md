## IV. CXCR6-PD-1+ TRM-like are highly functional T cells

To understand the divergent association of the TRM-like subtypes according to [[CXCR6]] and PD1 expression with [[ICB]] response, we formulated testable assumptions. We focused on the CXCR6-PD-1+ and the CXCR6+PD-1- population as they showed the most different results. First, since functional heterogeneity of [[TRM]] cells has been previously reported [[Cheuk_Immunity_2017]] [[Liikanen_JClinInvest_2021]] [[Mathew_JExpMed_2026]], we hypothesised that the TRM-like subtypes functionality could differ. Although both CXCR6+PD1- and CXCR6-PD1+ TRM-like cells produced similar level of IFN-γ and TNF-α, CXCR6-PD1+ TRM-like cells exhibited markedly higher [[IL-2]] secretion after anti-CD3/anti-CD28 stimulation (Figure 3 [[Figure 4 A.png|A]] and Extended Data Figure 3 [[Figure Supplémentaire 2 A.png|A]]). Notably, CXCR6-PD1+ TRM-like cells exhibited significantly higher expression of the [[TCR]] inducible co-stimulation receptors [[4-1BB]] and [[ICOS]], both under basal conditions and following activation in a kinetic _in vitro_ stimulation model (Figure 3 [[Figure 4 B.png|B]]-[[Figure 4 C.png|C]] and Extended Data Figure 2 [[Figure Supplémentaire 2 B.png|B]]).  We also assessed the cytotoxic potential using CD107a expression, and found that CXCR6-PD1+ TRM-like cells displayed higher membrane expression of CD107a compared with CXCR6+PD1- TRM-like cells, despite exhibiting comparable levels of granzyme B production (Figure 3 [[Figure 4 D.png|D]]-[[Figure 4 E.png|E]] and Extended Data Figure 2 [[Figure Supplémentaire 2 C.png|C]]). Together, these data suggests that CXCR6-PD1+ TRM-like cells might present higher functional capacity once recruited to the TME following [[ICB]] treatment.
